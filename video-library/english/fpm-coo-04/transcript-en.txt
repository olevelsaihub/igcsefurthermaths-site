Idea. A bus route follows a fixed road. A bus stop is on the route only if it is actually on that road. In coordinate geometry, a point lies on a line when its coordinates fit the line's equation. So to find a missing coordinate, substitute the known one into the equation. That leaves one unknown, and one unknown can be solved. Be careful which letter is x and which is y. Where a line crosses the y axis, x is zero. Where it crosses the x axis, y is zero. These are the intercepts. So substitute nought for the other letter. These questions say the point lies on the line, find the value of a, or show that a equals a given number, or write down where the line crosses an axis. They are usually the first one to three marks.

Method. Step one: write down the equation of the line. Step two: put the known coordinate in, or nought for an intercept. This leaves one unknown. Step three: solve the linear equation and write the value, or the full point if coordinates are asked. Step four: check by putting it back. The trap is swapping x and y.

Worked example. January twenty twenty. The line l one has equation y plus two x minus four equals zero. It passes through P, a, six, and Q, three, b. Find the value of a and the value of b. Two marks. For P, the y coordinate is six, so substitute y equals six. Six plus two a minus four equals zero. Simplify: two a plus two equals zero. So two a equals minus two, and a is minus one. For Q, the x coordinate is three. Substitute x equals three: b plus two times three, minus four, equals zero. That is b plus two equals zero, so b is minus two. Check on the graph. P is minus one, six, and Q is three, minus two. Both lie on the line. A wrong route puts six in for x, as if the point were six, a. That gives a equals minus eight, and the point minus eight, six, is not on the line. So a equals minus one and b equals minus two. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is a sign slip when moving terms across the equals sign. From two a plus two equals zero, some write two a equals two. The two moves across and becomes minus two.

Your turn. Now you try, from November twenty twenty five. The line L one has equation y minus three x plus a equals zero. The point A, a, ten, lies on L one. Show that a equals five. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. The point A is a, ten. So x is a and y is ten. Substitute into the equation: ten, minus three a, plus a, equals zero. Collect the a terms: minus three a plus a is minus two a. So ten minus two a equals zero. Then two a equals ten, and a equals five, as required. The most common wrong answer collects the a terms wrongly: minus three a plus a written as minus four a. That gives a equals two point five, not five. Minus three plus one is minus two. Remember: write the equation, substitute the known coordinate, solve, and write the answer in the form asked.
