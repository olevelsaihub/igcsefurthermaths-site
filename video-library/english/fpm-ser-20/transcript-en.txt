Method. Step one: set the bracket equal to the number, and solve for x. Step two: check x is in the valid range. Step three: substitute into every term, and add. Step four: round only at the end. A later part may scale it: the root of nineteen is five times the root of nought point seven six. The trap is putting x equal to the number itself. You solve for x first.
