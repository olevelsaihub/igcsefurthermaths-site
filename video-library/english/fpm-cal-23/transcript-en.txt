Idea. Think of a polo mint: a disc with a hole. Its volume is the whole disc minus the hole. Some volume questions need this: outer solid, minus inner solid. Spin the region between two graphs about the x-axis. Each thin slice is a ring, with outer radius y one and inner radius y two. The outer radius is further from the x-axis. The volume is pi times the integral of y one squared minus y two squared. Square each radius, then subtract. Do not subtract first. The limits are where the graphs meet. Test a value between them: the bigger y is the outer graph. These questions rotate a region between a curve and a line, or two curves, about the x-axis, and ask for the exact volume.

Method. Step one: solve the equations together for the limits. Step two: test a value between them to find the outer graph. Step three: work out pi times the integral of outer squared minus inner squared. Step four: integrate, use the limits, and simplify. The trap is squaring the difference. That is not the same thing.

Worked example. November twenty twenty one. The region enclosed by y equals four minus x squared and y equals x plus two is rotated about the x-axis. Find the exact volume. Six marks. Here is the region. Both graphs are above the x-axis, and the curve is on top. Step one: the limits. Four minus x squared equals x plus two gives x squared plus x minus two equals nought, so x is minus two or one. Step two: pi times the integral of outer squared minus inner squared. Do not square the difference. Square each one, then subtract. Expand: the first square is sixteen minus eight x squared plus x to the four. Subtract x squared plus four x plus four. That gives x to the four, minus nine x squared, minus four x, plus twelve. Step three: integrate. At one, the value is thirty six fifths. At minus two, it is minus seventy two fifths. Subtract: thirty six fifths plus seventy two fifths is one hundred and eight fifths. So V is one hundred and eight pi over five. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is a sign error with a negative limit. Minus two to the power five is minus thirty two, not plus.

Answer. Limits: nought and three over two. At x equals one, the curve is one and the line is a half, so the curve is outer. Integrate curve squared minus line squared. Integrate: x to the five over five, minus x to the four, plus five x cubed over four. At three over two, that is twenty seven over forty. So V is twenty seven pi over forty.
