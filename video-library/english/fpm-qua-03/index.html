Completing the square

Idea. Start with a square tile with side x. Its area is x squared. Add two strips, each three wide. They add six x. A three by three corner, area nine, is missing. Fill it, and you get a square with side x plus three. Completing the square means rewriting a quadratic as a bracket squared plus a number. Since x plus three squared is x squared plus six x plus nine, x squared plus six x is that, minus nine. The rule: halve the number in front of x, and put it in the bracket. Then subtract its square. Half of six is three, and three squared is nine. If there is a number in front of x squared, take it out of the x squared and x terms only. Then complete the square inside the bracket. The constant stays outside. In these questions, you write a quadratic in the form given, and find the three constants.

Method. Step one: take the number in front of x squared out of the x squared and x terms. Step two: halve the x-number inside, and put it in the bracket. Step three: subtract its square, and multiply by the number you took out. Step four: collect the plain numbers into one constant. Step five: check by expanding. The trap is forgetting to multiply the subtracted square by the number you took out.

Worked example. January twenty twenty three. f of x is eight x squared, plus ten x, minus three. Write it as A times x plus B, squared, plus C. Find A, B and C. Three marks. Step one: take eight out of the x squared and x terms. Ten divided by eight is five over four. The minus three stays outside. Step two: halve five over four to get five over eight. That goes in the bracket. Subtract its square, twenty five over sixty four. A tempting wrong route is to halve the ten. That gives x plus five, which is far too big. Always halve the number after you have taken the eight out. Step three: multiply the whole bracket by eight. Eight times twenty five over sixty four is twenty five over eight. So we have eight, times x plus five eighths squared, minus twenty five eighths, minus three. Step four: collect the numbers. Minus twenty five eighths minus twenty four eighths is minus forty nine eighths. So A is eight, B is five eighths, and C is minus forty nine eighths. Check with x equals two. The original gives forty nine. The new form gives four hundred and forty one over eight, minus forty nine over eight, also forty nine. Common mistake. A common mistake is a sign slip when x squared is negative. For minus x squared plus four x, take out minus one first. The answer has x minus two, not x plus two.

Your turn. Now you try, from January twenty twenty. Write f of x in the form a minus b times x plus c, squared. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. Put the terms in order: minus x squared, plus four x, plus seven. Take out minus one from the first two terms: minus, x squared minus four x, plus seven. Halve minus four to get minus two. So x squared minus four x is x minus two, squared, minus four. The minus sign outside means this minus four becomes plus four. Collect: four plus seven is eleven. So f of x is eleven minus, x minus two, squared. a is eleven, b is one, and c is minus two. The most common wrong answer writes x plus two. Its x term has the wrong sign. Remember: factor, halve, subtract, collect.
