Finding the equation of a tangent

Idea. Whirl a ball on a string, then let go. It flies off in a straight line that touches the circle at one point. In maths, that line is called a tangent. Gradient means steepness: how much y rises per step along x. A curve has a different gradient at each point: it is d y by d x at that x. The tangent has that same gradient. With the gradient m and the point, write the straight line: y minus y one equals m times x minus x one. These questions ask for the equation of a tangent, or where it meets an axis. On the x-axis, y is zero. On the y-axis, x is zero.

Method. Step one: find the point. If only x is given, put it into the curve for y. Step two: differentiate, then put x into d y by d x. That is the gradient. Step three: write y minus y one equals m times x minus x one. Step four: rearrange as asked, or put y or x equal to zero for an axis. The trap: using the height as the gradient. The curve gives the height. Only d y by d x gives the gradient.

Worked example. November twenty twenty. Curve C one is y equals five e to the minus two x, plus four. Curve C two is y equals e to the two x. They meet at A. The tangent at A to C one meets the x-axis at B. Show that the x coordinate of B is a half of five plus ln five. Five marks. Step one: the point. Part a finds A. Set the curves equal, let u be e to the two x, and solve: u is five. So x is a half ln five, and y is five. Step two: the gradient. Differentiate C one: minus ten e to the minus two x. At A, e to the minus two x is one fifth, so the gradient is minus two. Step three: the tangent. y minus five equals minus two times x minus a half ln five. Step four: B is on the x-axis, so y is zero. Minus five equals minus two times the bracket, so the bracket is five over two. Add a half ln five. x is five over two plus a half ln five, which is a half of five plus ln five. That is the printed result. A tempting wrong route is to use five, the height at A, as the gradient. The gradient must be d y by d x, which is minus two. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is a sign slip when finding the intercept. Minus five divided by minus two is positive.

Your turn. Now you try, from January twenty twenty two. The curve is y equals four root x, and A is nine, twelve. The tangent at A meets the x-axis at T. Find T. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. Write y as four x to the one half. The derivative is two x to the minus one half, which is two over root x. At x equals nine, that is two thirds. Tangent: y minus twelve equals two thirds times x minus nine. Put y equals zero. Then x minus nine is minus eighteen, so x is minus nine. T is minus nine, nought. The most common wrong answer loses the minus sign and gets twenty seven. But the gradient is positive, so T must be to the left of A. Remember: point, gradient from d y by d x, line through the point, then set y or x to zero for an axis.
