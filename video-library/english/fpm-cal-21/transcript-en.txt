Idea. Think of a garden with an odd shape. Cut it into easy pieces, find each area, and add. A region whose boundary changes part-way is solved the same way. A triangle is an easy piece. Its area is a half, times base, times height. A straight edge needs no integral. Here a curve is above the x-axis and a line is below it. Cut along the x-axis. The top piece is an integral. The bottom piece is a triangle. These questions give a region bounded by a curve, a line and an axis. Find the corners, split the region, find each area, and add.

Method. Step one: sketch the region and mark where its boundary changes. Step two: find the points you need, such as where lines meet the axes. Step three: integrate each curved piece, and use half base times height for each triangle. Step four: add the pieces. The trap is a missing piece, usually the triangle below the axis.

Worked example. January twenty twenty three. C is y equals four minus e to the two x. The line l is the normal to C at B. Region R is bounded by C, l and the y-axis. Find its area, to one decimal place. Seven marks. Part b gives l as y equals x over eight, minus l n two over eight. The curve is above the axis and the line is below it. Step one: split along the x-axis. The top piece is under C, from nought to l n two. The bottom piece is triangle O B D, where D is below O. Step two: the top piece. The integral is four l n two, minus three halves, which is one point two seven two six. Step three: the triangle. Half, times l n two, times l n two over eight, is l n two squared over sixteen. That is nought point zero three. A tempting route is to use only the area under the curve. That misses the triangle. It still rounds to one point three here, but it loses method marks. Step four: add. One point three zero two six, so one point three to one decimal place. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is the sign of D. The line has gradient one eighth through B, so going left it falls. D is below the axis, at minus l n two over eight.

Your turn. Now you try, from November twenty twenty. Region R is bounded by the x-axis, the curve S, y equals two sine x, and the curve C, y equals two cosine x. Find its area in the form a minus root b. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. A is where cosine x is nought, so x is pi over two. B is where sine x equals cosine x, so x is pi over four. Use S up to B, then C. Under S, minus two cosine x from nought to pi over four gives two minus root two. Under C, two sine x from pi over four to pi over two gives two minus root two. Add: four minus two root two. Two root two is root eight, so the area is four minus root eight. The common wrong answer integrates S all the way. After B the top edge is C, not S. Use each curve on its own interval. Remember: sketch, corners, integrate each curve piece, add. Use half base times height for straight pieces.
