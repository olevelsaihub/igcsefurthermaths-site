Idea. Take six plus nine. You can take out the common factor three, and write three times, two plus three. Taking out a factor means writing something as a factor times a bracket. The binomial series expands a bracket to a negative or fractional power. It only works if the bracket starts with one. So take the first number out. Two plus three x becomes two times, one plus three over two x. The key rule: the factor you take out gets the same power as the bracket. A negative power means a reciprocal, and a fractional power means a root. Two to the minus one is a half. Eight to the one third is two. The question says express in the form p times, one plus q x, to the power n. Find p and q.

Method. Step one: write any root or fraction as a single power. Step two: take the first number out, and divide the x term by it. Step three: give the power to both factors, and work out c to the power n. Step four: read off p and q. The traps: forgetting to give the outside factor the power, and forgetting to divide the x term.

Worked example. June twenty twenty two. Two plus three x, to the power minus one, is expressed as p times, one plus q x, to the power minus one. Find p and q. Two marks. Step one: take out the two. Two plus three x is two times, one plus three over two x. Step two: give the power to both factors. Two to the minus one, times one plus three over two x, to the minus one. Step three: two to the minus one is one half. So p is one half, and q is three over two. Check with x equals nought. The left side is two to the minus one, which is a half. The right side is a half times one, which is also a half. A tempting wrong route leaves the two without the power. At x equals nought that gives two, not a half. So it is wrong. So p is one half and q is three over two. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is to take two out but not divide the x term. At x equals one, the left side is a fifth, but the wrong form gives one eighth. Divide three x by two.

Your turn. Now you try, from January twenty twenty three. Eight plus three x, to the power one third, is written as p times, one plus q x, to the power one third. Find p and q. A power of a third means a cube root. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. Take eight out. Eight plus three x is eight times, one plus three over eight x. Give the power one third to both factors. Eight to the one third is the cube root of eight, which is two. So p is two and q is three over eight. Check with x equals nought: the cube root of eight is two, and two times one is two. The common wrong answer is p equals eight, forgetting the power. At x equals nought, the left side is two, not eight. Remember: take the first number out, divide the x term by it, give both factors the power, and read off p and q.
