Idea. A ball hits a flat wall at a right angle. On a curve, the line that touches it at a point is the tangent. The line at ninety degrees to it, through the same point, is the normal. Perpendicular lines have gradients that multiply to minus one. So the normal gradient is the negative reciprocal of the tangent gradient: flip it and change the sign. Then use y minus y one equals m times x minus x one with the normal gradient. Integer form, a x plus b y plus c equals nought, means clear the fractions by multiplying through. These questions ask for the equation of a normal, often in integer form. It all starts from the curve's gradient at the point.

Method. Step one: find the point on the curve. Step two: differentiate and put in x to get the tangent gradient m. The normal is built from this number. Step three: take the negative reciprocal. The trap is using m itself, or forgetting the minus sign. Step four: write y minus y one equals the normal gradient times x minus x one. Then rearrange to the form asked, clearing fractions for integers.

Worked example. A real question from June twenty twenty one. Find an equation of the normal to the curve y equals x cubed minus two x, all times e to the one minus x, at the point one, minus one. Five marks. Step one: check the point. At x equals one, x cubed minus two x is minus one, and e to the nought is one. So y is minus one. Step two: product rule. u is x cubed minus two x, with derivative three x squared minus two. v is e to the one minus x, with derivative minus e to the one minus x. Put in x equals one. The first part is one times one. The second part is minus, minus one, times one. So the tangent gradient is two. Now the normal. Take the negative reciprocal of two, which is minus a half. A tempting wrong route is to use two as it is. That is the tangent, not the normal. Write the line through one, minus one. y plus one equals minus a half times x minus one. Multiply by two. Two y plus two equals minus x plus one. So x plus two y plus one equals nought. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is using the tangent gradient for the normal, or the reciprocal without the minus sign. Neither line is perpendicular.

Your turn. Now you try, from June twenty nineteen. f of x is e to the three x times root one plus two x. Part a gives the derivative. In part b, find the normal at x equals nought, in integer form. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. At x equals nought, f is e to the nought times root one, which is one. So the point is nought, one. The derivative is two times one times two, over one, which is four. The normal gradient is minus one quarter. The line is y minus one equals minus a quarter times x. Multiply by four: four y minus four equals minus x. So x plus four y minus four equals nought. The most common wrong answer uses the tangent gradient, four, as it is. That is the tangent, so the normal marks are lost. Remember: point, tangent gradient, negative reciprocal, then the line in the form asked.
