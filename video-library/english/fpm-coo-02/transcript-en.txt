Idea. On a school trip, the teacher says you are three fifths of the way to the museum. A point that splits a line in a fixed ratio works the same way. A ratio compares two parts. The midpoint is the case of one to one. Its coordinates are the averages of the two x values and the two y values. For a ratio m to n, the point is m over m plus n of the way from A to B. The number next to A in the ratio is the number of parts from A. These questions give a ratio or the word midpoint, and ask for the coordinates of a point. Sometimes you work backwards to find an end point.

Method. Step one: find the order of the ratio. In A C to C B equals three to two, three is next to A. The total is five parts. Step two: find the change in x and in y from A to B. Step three: add three fifths of each change to A. Step four: for a missing end point, work backwards. The trap is putting the ratio the wrong way round.

Worked example. June twenty twenty four. A line passes through A, minus two, two, and B, three, twelve. The point C, p, q, lies on the line with A C to C B equal to three to two. Find p and q. Two marks. Step one: the ratio is three to two, so there are five parts. A C is three of them. So C is three fifths of the way from A to B. Step two: the change in x from A to B is three minus minus two, which is five. The change in y is twelve minus two, which is ten. Step three: p is minus two, plus three fifths of five. Three fifths of five is three. So p is one. Then q is two, plus three fifths of ten. Three fifths of ten is six. So q is eight. C is the point one, eight. Check with the formula: two times minus two, plus three times three, over five, is one. Two times two, plus three times twelve, over five, is eight. A wrong route uses two fifths, which gives the wrong point, zero, six. So p equals one and q equals eight. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is to divide by the wrong number. Three to two gives the fraction three over five, not three over two. The five is three plus two, the whole line.

Your turn. Now you try, from June twenty twenty one. A is minus four, minus ten. B is three, eleven. P lies on A B with A P to P B equal to three to four. Find P. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. Three plus four is seven parts, so P is three sevenths of the way. The change in x is three minus minus four, which is seven. The change in y is eleven minus minus ten, which is twenty one. Three sevenths of seven is three, so x is minus four plus three, which is minus one. Three sevenths of twenty one is nine, so y is minus ten plus nine, which is minus one. P is minus one, minus one. The most common wrong answer uses four sevenths, which gives zero, two. A P is the three part, next to A, so the fraction is three sevenths. Remember: find the total parts, the changes, and add the right fraction to the first point.
