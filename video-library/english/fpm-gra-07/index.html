Idea. A letter is a number we do not know yet. A clue is a fact the question gives you. I think of a number, double it, add three, and get eleven. Work backwards: the number is four. A rational curve is a fraction with x on the top and bottom. A vertical asymptote is a line the curve never touches. The bottom is zero there. If the asymptote is x equals minus four, x plus r is zero at minus four, so r is four. There are four kinds of clue. A horizontal asymptote means the ratio of the x coefficients. A y axis crossing means put x equals nought. An x axis crossing means the top is zero. This question gives a curve with letters, and some clues. Find the letters. Turn each clue into an equation and solve.

Method. Step one: list every asymptote and crossing given. Step two: the vertical asymptote makes the denominator zero at that x. Step three: the horizontal asymptote is the top x coefficient over the bottom one. An intercept gives another equation: put in the given point. Steps four and five: use any prime or integer condition, write the equation, and check. The trap is a sign slip. If the asymptote is x equals minus four, the bottom is x plus four.

Worked example. June twenty twenty five. C is y equals five x minus two, over, a x plus b. Its vertical asymptote is x equals minus four thirds. It crosses the y axis at nought, minus a quarter. Show that b is eight, then find a. Three marks. Step one: list the clues. The asymptote means the bottom is zero at x equals minus four thirds. The y crossing means x is nought and y is minus a quarter. Step two: put x equals nought. The top is minus two, the bottom is b. Minus two over b equals minus a quarter, so b is eight, as required. Step three: the bottom, a x plus eight, is zero at x equals minus four thirds. So minus four a over three, plus eight, is nought. a is six. A tempting wrong route is writing b over a as minus four thirds. That gives a equals minus six. But the asymptote is minus b over a, so a is six. Check the final equation. At x equals nought, y is minus two over eight, which is minus a quarter. And the bottom is zero at x equals minus four thirds. So b is eight, shown, and a is six. Common mistake. A common mistake is dropping the minus sign from the given y value. Minus two over b equals plus a quarter gives b equals minus eight. Keep the sign the question gives.

Your turn. Now you try, from January twenty twenty three. The curve is y equals a x plus three, over, one minus two x. Its horizontal asymptote is y equals four. Find a. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. The horizontal asymptote is the top x coefficient over the bottom one. The top has a. The bottom, one minus two x, has x coefficient minus two. So a over minus two is four. Multiply both sides by minus two. a is minus eight. Check: for huge x, minus eight x over minus two x is four. The most common wrong answer ignores the sign and uses two. Then a is eight. The bottom's x coefficient is minus two. Remember: list the clues, turn each into an equation, solve, and check.
