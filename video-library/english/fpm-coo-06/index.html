Equation of a perpendicular line (including perpendicular bisectors)

Idea. A road crosses a railway at right angles. The road is perpendicular to the railway. A perpendicular gradient is the negative reciprocal: flip it, and change the sign. A perpendicular bisector cuts a line segment in half, at a right angle. So it passes through the midpoint, and its gradient is the negative reciprocal of the segment's gradient. Any line needs a gradient and a point. For a perpendicular line, the gradient comes from the perpendicular rule. The point is given, or is the midpoint, or comes from a ratio. These questions say a line is perpendicular to another and passes through a point. Often the equation is printed, so you must reach it exactly.

Method. Step one: find the gradient of the given line or segment. Step two: take the negative reciprocal. Step three: find the point the new line goes through. Step four: put the gradient and the point into y minus y one equals m, x minus x one. Clear fractions and rearrange to the form asked. The trap is using the wrong point, or the wrong gradient.

Worked example. January twenty twenty two. L one is y minus two x minus six equals zero. The point P, two, a, lies on L one. L two is perpendicular to L one and goes through P. Show that an equation of L two is x plus two y minus twenty two equals zero. Four marks. First, the point P. Put x equals two into L one: a minus four minus six equals zero. So a is ten, and P is two, ten. Now the gradient. Rearrange L one to y equals two x plus six. Its gradient is two. The perpendicular gradient is minus one half. Now the equation. Y minus ten equals minus one half, times, x minus two. This line goes through P with the right gradient. Multiply by two to clear the fraction: two y minus twenty equals minus x plus two. Move everything to one side: x plus two y minus twenty two equals zero. That is the printed equation. A tempting wrong route is to use the reciprocal only, one half. Then the line is x minus two y plus eighteen equals zero, which is not the printed equation. So an equation of L two is x plus two y minus twenty two equals zero, as required. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is doing half the rule. Some students only change the sign, or only flip. The perpendicular gradient to two is minus one half. Both steps are needed.

Your turn. Now you try, from January twenty twenty three. The line l has gradient minus one twelfth and passes through A, three, ten. The line k is perpendicular to l and passes through A. Find an equation for k in the form y equals m x plus c. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. The gradient of l is minus one twelfth. Flip it to get minus twelve, then change the sign, to get plus twelve. So the gradient of k is twelve. The point A is three, ten. Use y minus ten equals twelve, times, x minus three. Multiply out: y minus ten equals twelve x minus thirty six. So y equals twelve x minus twenty six. Check A: twelve times three, minus twenty six, is ten. The most common wrong answer only changes the sign, using one twelfth. That line is not perpendicular. Flip and change the sign. Remember: gradient, negative reciprocal, a point, then the equation in the form asked.
