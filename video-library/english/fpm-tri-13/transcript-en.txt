Idea. A tent pole stands straight up from the middle of the floor. A rope runs from the top of the pole to a corner. The pole, the floor and the rope make a right-angled triangle. A solid is a three dimensional shape. Inside it you can always find a flat right-angled triangle, and Pythagoras works in it. A rectangle with sides a and b has a diagonal of root of a squared plus b squared. In a right pyramid, the top point, called the vertex, is directly above the centre of the base. That is where the two diagonals cross. The centre is halfway along each diagonal. So this type asks for a length in a solid. Find a right-angled triangle, draw it flat, and use Pythagoras.

Method. Step one: find a right-angled triangle that contains the length you want, and draw it flat. Step two: work out the base lengths first, like half the base diagonal. Step three: use Pythagoras, or a trig ratio if an angle is given. Step four: keep surds exact. A rounded value cannot reach a printed answer. The trap is using the whole diagonal where half is needed.

Worked example. November twenty twenty five. Figure four shows a right pyramid A B C D E. The base is a horizontal square of side six centimetres. The sloping edges are all twelve centimetres. The height is h. Show that h is three root fourteen. Three marks. Step one: the base diagonal. A C is the square root of six squared plus six squared. That is root seventy two, which is six root two. Step two: let O be the centre. The vertex is above O, so E O is vertical. A O is half of A C, which is three root two. Triangle E O A is right-angled at O. Step three: Pythagoras in triangle E O A. h squared is twelve squared minus eighteen. That is one hundred and twenty six. Then h is root one hundred and twenty six. Step four: one hundred and twenty six is nine times fourteen. So root one hundred and twenty six is three root fourteen. That is the answer we were asked to show. A tempting route uses the whole diagonal. Then h squared is one hundred and forty four minus seventy two, which is seventy two. That gives six root two, not three root fourteen. So h is three root fourteen. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is treating the sloping edge as the height. The sloping edge is the longest side of the triangle. So the height must be shorter than twelve.
