Idea. A cinema has two seats in row one, seven in row two, twelve in row three. Every row has five more. How many seats are in the first n rows? Adding them all up is a sum. Sigma means add up. Sigma from r equals one to n of five r minus three says: put r equal to one, two, three, up to n, and add. That gives two plus seven plus twelve, and so on. A rule like five r minus three always gives an arithmetic series. The first term a is the value at r equals one. The common difference d is the number in front of r. There are n terms. Then use the sum formula. The sum of n terms, called S n, is n over two, times, two a plus n minus one times d. You must know this formula. These questions say show that. The sum is printed, so every line must be written. You end exactly at the printed result.

Method. Step one: put r equal to one, two, three to see the first terms. Step two: state a and d, and say there are n terms. Step three: put a, d and n into the sum formula. Step four: simplify inside the bracket, line by line, to the printed answer. The trap is the first term. Here the number added is minus three, but the first term is two.

Worked example. June twenty twenty four, paper one. Show that the sum from r equals one to n of five r minus three equals n over two, times five n minus one. Three marks. Step one: put r equal to one, two, three. We get two, seven and twelve. The jump is five, so the series is arithmetic. The first term a is two, the common difference d is five, and there are n terms. Step three: write the formula and substitute. N over two, times, two times two, plus n minus one times five. Step four: simplify inside the bracket. Four plus five n minus five is five n minus one. Reject a tempting route. Taking the first term as minus three gives n over two, times five n minus eleven. That does not match the printed answer. Check with n equal to four. Two plus seven plus twelve plus seventeen is thirty eight. The formula gives four over two times nineteen, also thirty eight. The sum equals n over two, times five n minus one, as required. Common mistake. Another mistake is the last term. If you use the sum of first plus last, the last term is the value at r equals n, which is five n minus three. Not five n.

Your turn. Now you try, from June twenty twenty three. Show that the sum from r equals one to n of three r plus two equals n over two, times three n plus seven. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. Put r equal to one, two, three. We get five, eight, eleven. So a is five and d is three, with n terms. Substitute into the formula. N over two, times ten plus three n minus three. That simplifies to n over two, times three n plus seven. The common wrong answer takes the first term as two. That gives n over two times three n plus one. It is not the printed result. Remember the method. Write the terms, state a, d and n, substitute, and simplify.
