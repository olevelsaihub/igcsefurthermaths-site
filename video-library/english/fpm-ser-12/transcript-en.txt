Idea. Imagine walking towards a wall, each step half as long as the last. Eight metres, four, two, one. You get closer and closer, but never pass a fixed distance. If adding more terms gets closer and closer to a fixed number, the series is convergent. That number is the sum to infinity. If the total keeps growing, the series is not convergent. For a geometric series it depends on the common ratio, r. If the size of r is less than one, the terms shrink. The size of r, written with two bars, is called the modulus. It means r without its sign. Four examples. A half shrinks. Minus a half swings up and down but shrinks. Three grows. Minus two swings and grows. The sign does not matter. Only the size does. These questions ask you to show a series is convergent, or explain why it has no sum to infinity. Find r, compare its size with one, and write a sentence.

Method. Step one: find r. Divide the second term by the first. Check by dividing the third by the second. They must agree. Step two: find the size of r and compare it with one. If it is less than one, the series is convergent. If it is one or more, it is not. Step three: write a sentence with your value of r, how its size compares with one, and your conclusion.

Worked example. November twenty twenty, paper two. The first three terms are x minus three, x plus one, and four x minus two. From part a, x is one third or five. Given x is less than one, show the series is convergent. Two marks. Step one: x is less than one, so x is one third. The terms are minus eight thirds, four thirds, minus two thirds. Step two: find r. Second over first is four thirds over minus eight thirds, which is minus a half. Third over second is also minus a half. Step three: compare. The size of r is one half, which is less than one. So the series is convergent. Reject a tempting route. Using x equal to five gives terms two, six, eighteen, so r is three. That is not convergent. This is why x is less than one. So the exam answer is: r is minus a half, so the size of r is less than one, so the series is convergent. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is to say r is less than one. Minus two is less than one, but a series with ratio minus two is not convergent. Use the size of r.

Your turn. Now you try, from November twenty twenty five. A geometric series G has first term three plus root three. The second term is root three. Explain why G is convergent. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. First find r. Divide the second term by the first: root three over three plus root three. Multiply top and bottom by three minus root three. The bottom is nine minus three, which is six. So r is root three minus one, over two. Root three is about one point seven three. So r is about nought point three seven. The exam answer: the size of r is less than one, so G is convergent. The common wrong answer divides the wrong way: first term over second. That gives root three plus one, about two point seven three, so it says G is not convergent. Always divide a term by the one before it. Remember the method. Find r, find its size, compare with one, and write the sentence.
