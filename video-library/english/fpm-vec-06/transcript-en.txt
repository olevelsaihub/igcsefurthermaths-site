Idea. Think of a door frame pushed sideways. Top and bottom are parallel, and so are the sides. That shape is a parallelogram: both pairs of opposite sides are parallel and equal. Vectors can prove it. Parallel vectors point the same way, or opposite ways. One is a number times the other. Six i plus nine j is three times two i plus three j, so they are parallel. If one pair of opposite sides are equal vectors, the shape is a parallelogram. A trapezium has just one pair of parallel sides, so also show the other pair is not parallel. This type asks you to prove a shape, or show three points are collinear, which means on one straight line. Give the vectors, the multiple, and a conclusion.

Method. Step one: decide which vectors to compare: opposite sides for a shape, or two vectors from a common point for collinear points. Step two: find each, end minus start. Step three: show one vector is a number times the other. A parallelogram needs equal vectors. A trapezium needs the other pair shown not parallel. Step five: write a conclusion sentence. The trap is writing they are parallel with no working, or forgetting the final sentence.

Worked example. January twenty twenty. Vector A B is seven i plus p j, A C is eleven i minus p j, and A D is four i minus two p j. Show that, for all p, A B C D is a parallelogram. Three marks. We compare opposite sides. A D is given. We need B C, so we can compare A D with B C. Vector B C is end minus start: A C minus A B. Eleven i minus p j, minus, seven i plus p j, gives four i minus two p j. Compare. Vector A D is four i minus two p j. That is exactly vector B C. So B C equals A D. Equal vectors have the same direction and length. So A D and B C are parallel and equal. This holds whatever p is, because p appears in both. A tempting wrong route is to test one value, like p equals one. That only proves the shape for p equals one. The question says all values of p. So B C equals A D: opposite sides are parallel and equal. So A B C D is a parallelogram for all p. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is writing that two sides are parallel, with no working. The marks are for the vectors, the multiple, and the conclusion.

Your turn. Now you try, from November twenty twenty five. A B is two a plus four b. A C is seven a plus seven b. A D is ten a plus six b. Show that A B C D is a trapezium. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. Vector B C is A C minus A B: five a plus three b. Vector A D is ten a plus six b, which is two times B C. So A D is parallel to B C. Vector D C is A C minus A D: minus three a plus b. A multiple of A B would need k equal to minus three halves, and one quarter. Impossible, so they are not parallel. So exactly one pair is parallel, and A B C D is a trapezium. The most common wrong answer stops after A D equals two B C. The second pair must be tested too. Remember: choose the pairs, find the vectors, show the multiple, and write the conclusion.
