Idea. Think of the two set squares in a pencil case. One has angles of forty five and forty five. The other has thirty and sixty. The sine, cosine and tangent of these angles have exact values, written with roots. An exact value keeps the root, like root two. It is not a calculator decimal. A root that does not simplify is called a surd. Other angles are built from these. Seventy five is forty five plus thirty. Fifteen is forty five minus thirty. Tangent repeats every one hundred and eighty degrees, so tangent of two hundred and fifty five equals tangent of seventy five. So the question gives a surd, and you show it is the exact value. Split the angle, use the formula, put in the exact values, and simplify.

Method. Step one: write the angle as a sum or difference of thirty, forty five and sixty. Reduce a large angle first. Step two: choose the matching formula from page two. Step three: put in the exact values, each in brackets. Step four: multiply out and tidy. Root two times root three is root six. The trap is a sign slip. Cosine of A plus B has a minus in the middle. Cosine of A minus B has a plus.

Worked example. June twenty twenty four. Figure five shows a right triangular prism. Angle A B F is forty five degrees, and angle B A F is sixty degrees. Using a formula from page two, show that sine A F B is root two plus root six, over four. Three marks. Angles in a triangle add to one hundred and eighty. So angle A F B is one hundred and eighty minus sixty minus forty five. That is seventy five degrees. Seventy five is forty five plus thirty. Write the sine addition formula with A as forty five and B as thirty. Step three: put in the exact values. Sine and cosine of forty five are both root two over two. Cosine of thirty is root three over two. Sine of thirty is one half. Multiply. Root two times root three is root six, so the first term is root six over four. The second is root two over four. Adding gives the printed result. A tempting route adds the sines: sine forty five plus sine thirty. That is about one point two one. A sine is never more than one, so this is wrong. So write the angle, the formula, the exact values, and the printed result. This explanation is by olevels dot com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common slip is adding under the root. Root two times root three is root six, not root five.

Your turn. Now you try, from January twenty twenty. Show the exact value of tangent two hundred and fifty five. Write your answer, then choose Continue to Answer.

Answer. Tangent repeats every one hundred and eighty degrees, so tangent two fifty five is tangent seventy five. Use the tangent formula with forty five plus thirty. Tangent forty five is one. Tangent thirty is one over root three. Step four: multiply the top and the bottom by three. Three over root three is root three. That gives the printed result. A common wrong answer puts a plus on the bottom of the tangent formula. The fraction becomes one, but tangent seventy five is about three point seven three. Remember: split the angle, choose the formula, put in exact values, simplify.
