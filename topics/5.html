Series · Type 1

Does a geometric series have a sum to infinity?

Stating or showing that a geometric series has a sum to infinity because |r| < 1, or has none because |r| > 1.

Series · Type 6

Adding up a finite geometric series

Using S_n = a(1 - r^n)/(1 - r) (including for a sigma sum) to find or show a sum, often in exact or surd form.
