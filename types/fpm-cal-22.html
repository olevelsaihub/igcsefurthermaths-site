Volume of revolution about the x-axis

Calculation

Rotating about the $x$-axis gives $V = \pi\int y^2\,dx$: square the whole expression first, then use an identity to remove any trig square.

Square $y$ in full, then rewrite any $\sin^2 x$ or $\cos^2 x$ with a double-angle identity.

5

The method

  1. Square y in full

    Expand the whole bracket: $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$.

  2. Replace trig squares

    Use $\sin^2 x = \frac{1}{2}(1 - \cos 2x)$ or $\cos^2 x = \frac{1}{2}(1 + \cos 2x)$.

  3. Integrate between the limits

    Upper limit minus lower limit.

  4. Multiply by π

    Keep the answer exact, in terms of π if asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Partial squaring

    $(3 + 2\sin x)^2 \neq 9 + 4\sin^2 x$: keep the middle term.

  • ✗
    Forgetting π

    The formula always has a factor $\pi$.

Volume of revolution about the x-axis · June 2022 · Paper 2 · Question 6 · part (b) · 6 marks

The finite region $R$ is bounded by the curve with equation $y = 3 + 2\sin x$, the $x$-axis, the $y$-axis and the line with equation $x = \frac{\pi}{4}$. The region $R$ is rotated through $360°$ about the $x$-axis. Use calculus to find the volume of the solid generated, giving your answer to the nearest integer.

Worked explanation

  1. $V = \pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}(3 + 2\sin x)^2\,dx = \pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}(9 + 12\sin x + 4\sin^2 x)\,dx$
  2. $4\sin^2 x = 2(1 - \cos 2x) = 2 - 2\cos 2x$, so the integrand is $11 + 12\sin x - 2\cos 2x$.
  3. $V = \pi\left[11x - 12\cos x - \sin 2x\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}$
  4. $= \pi\left[\left(\frac{11\pi}{4} - 12 \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - 1\right) - (0 - 12 - 0)\right] = \pi\left(\frac{11\pi}{4} + 11 - 6\sqrt{2}\right)$
  5. $= \pi(8.6394 + 11 - 8.4853) = \pi(11.1541\ldots) = 35.04\ldots$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$V \approx 35$ (nearest integer)

How the marks are earned

A method mark for $\pi\int y^2\,dx$ with $(3 + 2\sin x)^2$ expanded, a method mark for replacing $\sin^2 x$ using $\cos 2x$, method and accuracy marks for integrating, a method mark for substituting $0$ and $\frac{\pi}{4}$, and accuracy for 35.
