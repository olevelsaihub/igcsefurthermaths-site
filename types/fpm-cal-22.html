Knowledge card

Volume of revolution about the x-axis

Calculation
  • Rotating $y = f(x)$ from $x = a$ to $x = b$ through $360°$ about the $x$-axis gives volume $V = \pi\int_a^b y^2\,dx$.
  • Square the whole expression: $(3 + 2\sin x)^2 = 9 + 12\sin x + 4\sin^2 x$, not $9 + 4\sin^2 x$.
  • Use $\sin^2 x = \frac{1}{2}(1 - \cos 2x)$ or $\cos^2 x = \frac{1}{2}(1 + \cos 2x)$ before integrating.
  • $\int e^{kx}\,dx = \frac{1}{k}e^{kx}$, so $(e^{3x})^2 = e^{6x}$ integrates to $\frac{1}{6}e^{6x}$.
  • If the volume is given, set $\pi\int y^2\,dx$ equal to it and solve for the unknown limit.

Before you begin

  • Expanding brackets.
  • Double-angle identities.
  • Integration of trig and exponential functions.
1

The method

  1. Square $y$ carefully, expanding brackets fully.

    Why: The formula needs $y^2$; partial squaring loses the cross term.

  2. Rewrite any $\sin^2$ or $\cos^2$ with the double-angle identity.

    Why: Squares of trig functions cannot be integrated directly.

  3. Integrate and substitute the limits.

    Why: This gives the integral's value.

  4. Multiply by $\pi$ (or set equal to the given volume and solve).

    Why: Forgetting $\pi$ is a common slip.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Squaring term by term and losing the cross term.
  • ✗
    Forgetting the factor $\pi$.
  • ✗
    Integrating $\sin^2 x$ without the identity.
  • ✗
    Using degrees or a decimal too early instead of exact values at the limits.

Volume of revolution about the x-axis · June 2022 · Paper 2 · Question 6 · part (b) · 6 marks

The finite region $R$ is bounded by the curve with equation $y = 3 + 2\sin x$, the $x$-axis, the $y$-axis and the line with equation $x = \frac{\pi}{4}$. The region $R$ is rotated through $360°$ about the $x$-axis. Use calculus to find the volume of the solid generated, giving your answer to the nearest integer.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $V = \pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}(3 + 2\sin x)^2\,dx = \pi\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}(9 + 12\sin x + 4\sin^2 x)\,dx$
  2. $4\sin^2 x = 2(1 - \cos 2x) = 2 - 2\cos 2x$, so the integrand is $11 + 12\sin x - 2\cos 2x$.
  3. $V = \pi\left[11x - 12\cos x - \sin 2x\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}$
  4. $= \pi\left[\left(\frac{11\pi}{4} - 12 \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - 1\right) - (0 - 12 - 0)\right] = \pi\left(\frac{11\pi}{4} + 11 - 6\sqrt{2}\right)$
  5. $= \pi(8.6394 + 11 - 8.4853) = \pi(11.1541\ldots) = 35.04\ldots$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$V \approx 35$ (nearest integer)

How the marks are earned

A method mark for $\pi\int y^2\,dx$ with $(3 + 2\sin x)^2$ expanded, a method mark for replacing $\sin^2 x$ using $\cos 2x$, method and accuracy marks for integrating, a method mark for substituting $0$ and $\frac{\pi}{4}$, and accuracy for 35.

Calculation check

  • round(3.141592653589793 × (11 × 3.141592653589793 ÷ 4 + 11 - 6 × 2 × × 0.5), 2) = 35.04
  • round(3.141592653589793 × (11 × 3.141592653589793 ÷ 4 + 11 - 6 × 2 × × 0.5)) = 35
