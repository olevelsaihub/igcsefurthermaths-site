Knowledge card

Using the cosine rule, including when it gives a quadratic

Calculation

The cosine rule $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$ finds a side from two sides and the included angle, or an angle from three sides. With sides in $x$ it gives a quadratic.

2

The calculation

$a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$, so $\cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc}$

The angle $A$ sits between sides $b$ and $c$, and $a$ is opposite it.

5

The method

  1. Label the triangle

    Find the side and the angle opposite it, and the two sides that meet at the angle.

  2. Write the rule

    Write $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$ with the values in place.

  3. Simplify to a quadratic

    Expand every bracket carefully and collect terms until one side is zero.

  4. Solve and reject

    Solve the quadratic and reject any root that makes a side zero or negative.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong side opposite

    The side on the left must be the one opposite the angle.

  • ✗
    Expansion slips

    $(3x - 1)^2 = 9x^2 - 6x + 1$, not $9x^2 - 1$.

In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 5x$ cm, $BC = (3x - 1)$ cm, $AC = (2x + 5)$ cm and angle $ABC = 60^\circ$. Find, to 3 significant figures, the value of $x$.

Worked explanation

  1. Angle $B = 60^\circ$ is between $AB$ and $BC$; the side opposite is $AC$.
  2. $(2x + 5)^2 = (5x)^2 + (3x - 1)^2 - 2(5x)(3x - 1)\cos 60^\circ$
  3. $4x^2 + 20x + 25 = 25x^2 + 9x^2 - 6x + 1 - 5x(3x - 1)$
  4. $4x^2 + 20x + 25 = 34x^2 - 6x + 1 - 15x^2 + 5x = 19x^2 - x + 1$
  5. $0 = 15x^2 - 21x - 24 \Rightarrow 5x^2 - 7x - 8 = 0$
  6. $x = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{49 + 160}}{10} = \frac{7 \pm \sqrt{209}}{10}$, so $x = 2.1457$ or $x = -0.7457$
  7. A negative $x$ gives negative sides, so $x = 2.15$ (3 s.f.). Check: $BC = 3(2.1457) - 1 > 0$.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

x = 2.15

How the marks are earned

A method mark for a correct cosine-rule statement, a method mark for expanding and simplifying to a three-term quadratic, accuracy for 5x^2 - 7x - 8 = 0, a method mark for solving, and accuracy for 2.15 with the negative root rejected.
