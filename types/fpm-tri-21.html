Exact values with the addition formulae

Finding exact trig values of angles such as 15, 75, 105 or 255 degrees by writing them as sums or differences of 30, 45 and 60 degrees.

Type 4 10 Trigonometry FPM-TRI-21

★☆☆☆☆ · 2 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

'Without a calculator' or 'using exact values', show that $\sin 75^\circ$, $\cos 15^\circ$, $\tan 255^\circ$ etc. equals a given surd expression.

Minus inside the angle

Why

Trap

5

The method

  1. Split the angle

    Write the angle as a sum or difference of $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$ and $60^\circ$.

  2. Choose the formula

    Pick $\sin(A + B)$, $\cos(A - B)$ or the matching formula from the sheet.

  3. Substitute exact values

    Use $\sin 45^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, $\cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ and so on.

  4. Simplify to the form asked

    Multiply out and collect the surds over a common denominator.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Sign error

    Check the sign in the formula before substituting.

  • ✗
    Calculator decimal

    A decimal value is not a proof of an exact answer.

Exact values with the addition formulae · June 2024 · Paper 2 · Question 9 · part (a) · 3 marks

In the right triangular prism $ABCDEF$, triangle $ABF$ has $\angle BAF = 60^\circ$ and $\angle ABF = 45^\circ$, with $AB = 24$ cm. Using a formula from page 2, (a) show that $\sin AFB = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$.

Worked explanation

  1. $\angle AFB = 180^\circ - 60^\circ - 45^\circ = 75^\circ$
  2. $\sin 75^\circ = \sin(45^\circ + 30^\circ) = \sin 45^\circ\cos 30^\circ + \cos 45^\circ\sin 30^\circ$
  3. $= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$
  4. $\sin AFB = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$\sin AFB = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for angle AFB = 75 degrees written as 45 + 30, a method mark for the sine addition formula with exact values, and an accuracy mark for the simplified result.
