Trig equations that factorise

Solving an equation by factorising out a common trig factor (or using a product form) and solving each factor, instead of dividing and losing solutions.

Type 18 10 Trigonometry FPM-TRI-26

★★☆☆☆ · 7 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

An equation such as $2\tan y + 5\sin y = 0$ or $\sin 2x - \tan x = 0$ where every term shares a trig factor, often with 'giving your solutions to one decimal place where appropriate' (hinting that some answers are exact).

Trig equations that factorise

Calculation

Move every term to one side and factorise rather than divide by a trig factor. Dividing by $\sin x$ loses the solutions where $\sin x = 0$.

Tan as sin over cos

Why
$\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, so both sides share $\sin x$.

Keep the common factor

Trap
Dividing both sides by $\sin x$ loses $x = 0$ and $180$.

Solve each factor

Working
$\sin x = 0$ gives $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$.

Impossible factor

Scores
$\cos x = \frac{3}{2}$ has no solution, so it adds nothing.

The calculation

If $AB = 0$, then $A = 0$ or $B = 0$

$\sin x = 0$ at $0^\circ$, $180^\circ$ and $360^\circ$; $\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$ is undefined when $\cos x = 0$.

5

The method

  1. Get zero on one side

    Move every term to the left-hand side so the right is zero.

  2. Factorise

    Take out the common trig factor, such as $\sin x$.

  3. Solve each factor

    Set each factor equal to zero and solve it over the interval.

  4. Collect and check

    Combine the solutions and check each lies in the given range.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dividing by a factor

    Dividing by $\sin x$ loses the roots where $\sin x = 0$.

  • ✗
    Undefined values

    Remove any value where $\tan x$ or another ratio is undefined.

(b) Giving your solutions to one decimal place where appropriate, solve the equation $2\tan y^\circ + 5\sin y^\circ = 0$ for $0 \le y \le 180$.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $\frac{2\sin y}{\cos y} + 5\sin y = 0$
  2. $\sin y\left(\frac{2}{\cos y} + 5\right) = 0$ (multiplying by $\cos y$: $\sin y(2 + 5\cos y) = 0$)
  3. $\sin y = 0 \Rightarrow y = 0$ or $y = 180$
  4. $2 + 5\cos y = 0 \Rightarrow \cos y = -\frac{2}{5}$
  5. $y = \cos^{-1}(-0.4) = 180^\circ - 66.42^\circ = 113.6^\circ$ (the only value in $0 \le y \le 180$)
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$y = 0$, $y = 113.6$, $y = 180$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for writing tan as sin/cos and factorising out sin y, an accuracy mark for y = 0 and 180, a method mark for solving cos y = -0.4, and an accuracy mark for 113.6.

Calculation check

  • -2 ÷ 5 = -0.4
  • round(180-66.42182152179817,1) = 113.6
