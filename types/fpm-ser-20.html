Knowledge card

Using a binomial expansion to estimate a value

Calculation
  • Choose $x$ so that the bracket becomes the number you want, e.g. $1 - 4x = 0.76$ gives $x = 0.06$.
  • The chosen $x$ must be inside the validity range, and a small $x$ gives a better estimate.
  • Substitute into the expansion (not the original expression) and keep full accuracy until the end.
  • Related values can be found by scaling: $\sqrt{19} = \sqrt{25 \times 0.76} = 5\sqrt{0.76}$.
  • Percentage error $= \frac{|\text{estimate} - \text{true value}|}{\text{true value}} \times 100$.

Before you begin

  • Binomial expansions.
  • Decimal arithmetic and rounding.
  • Surd manipulation.
1

The method

  1. Set the bracket (or the whole expression) equal to the number to be estimated and solve for x.

    Why: That x makes the series equal to the wanted value.

  2. Check the x is in the valid range.

    Why: Otherwise the series does not converge to the value.

  3. Substitute x into each term of the expansion and add.

    Why: The question asks for the series estimate, not a calculator value.

  4. Round only at the end to the accuracy asked.

    Why: Early rounding can change the final digit.

  5. If needed, scale the estimate to the related value (or compare with the true value).

    Why: Follow-on parts often build on this estimate.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Choosing an x outside the valid range, or setting x equal to the number itself.
  • ✗
    Rounding each term too early.
  • ✗
    Giving the calculator value with no series working.
  • ✗
    Scaling by the wrong factor in the follow-on part.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from typical examiner credit patterns for this method; not measured student-error statistics.

Now see it used on a real past-paper question

What a complete response includes

A method mark for a correct and suitable value of x, a method mark for substituting it into the expansion, and an accuracy mark for the value correct to the stated accuracy. Follow-on parts give a method mark for the correct scaling and an accuracy mark for the result.

Using a binomial expansion to estimate a value · January 2020 · Paper 1 · Question 9 · part (b) · 3 marks

Given that $\sqrt{1 - 4x} = 1 - 2x - 2x^2 - 4x^3 + \dots$, (b) use your expansion with a suitable value for $x$ to obtain an estimate of $\sqrt{0.76}$. Give your answer correct to 4 decimal places.

Question restated by olevels.com from the original paper; the original is below.

View full original question and diagrams
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $1 - 4x = 0.76 \Rightarrow 4x = 0.24 \Rightarrow x = 0.06$, which is inside $|x| < \frac{1}{4}$
  2. $\sqrt{0.76} \approx 1 - 2(0.06) - 2(0.06)^2 - 4(0.06)^3$
  3. $= 1 - 0.12 - 0.0072 - 0.000864$
  4. $= 0.871936$
  5. $\approx 0.8719$ (4 d.p.)
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$\sqrt{0.76} \approx 0.8719$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for x = 0.06, a method mark for substituting it into the expansion, and an accuracy mark for 0.8719.

Calculation check

  • 1-4 × 0.06 = 0.76
  • round(1-2 × 0.06-2 × 0.06 × × 2-4 × 0.06 × × 3,6) = 0.871936
  • round(1-2 × 0.06-2 × 0.06 × × 2-4 × 0.06 × × 3,4) = 0.8719
