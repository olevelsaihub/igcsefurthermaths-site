Regions made by tangents and a sector

Calculation

A tangent meets the radius at $90^\circ$, so the tangent length is $r\tan\theta$. The shaded region is the triangle minus the sector.

The calculation

Tangent length $BC = r\tan\theta$, and shaded area $= \frac{1}{2}r^2(\tan\theta - \theta)$

5

The method

  1. Mark the right angle

    The tangent meets the radius at $90^\circ$ where they touch.

  2. Find the tangent length

    Use $\tan\theta = \frac{BC}{r}$ in the right-angled triangle.

  3. Find both areas

    Triangle $= \frac{1}{2} \times r \times BC$ and sector $= \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$.

  4. Subtract

    The shaded area is the triangle minus the sector.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Radius as tangent

    The tangent length is $r\tan\theta$, not $r$.

  • ✗
    Degrees in the sector

    Convert the angle to radians before using $\frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$.

Regions made by tangents and a sector · January 2020 · Paper 1 · Question 4 · part (whole) · 6 marks

Sector $AOB$ has centre $O$, radius $r$ cm and angle $0.8$ radians. Points $O$, $A$ and $C$ lie on a straight line so that $CB$ is the tangent to the circle at $B$. The shaded region (bounded by $AC$, $CB$ and the arc $AB$) has area $101$ cm$^2$. Find $r$, correct to 3 significant figures.

Worked explanation

  1. $CB$ is a tangent, so $\angle OBC = \frac{\pi}{2}$.
  2. In triangle $OBC$: $BC = r\tan 0.8$
  3. Area of triangle $OBC = \frac{1}{2} \times r \times r\tan 0.8 = \frac{1}{2}r^2\tan 0.8$
  4. Area of sector $AOB = \frac{1}{2}r^2(0.8) = 0.4r^2$
  5. Shaded area: $\frac{1}{2}r^2\tan 0.8 - 0.4r^2 = 101$
  6. $\tan 0.8 = 1.02964$ (radians), so $r^2(0.51482 - 0.4) = 101 \Rightarrow r^2 = \frac{101}{0.11482} = 879.6$
  7. $r = 29.66$, so $r = 29.7$ (3 s.f.)
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

r = 29.7

How the marks are earned

Method marks for BC = r tan 0.8, for the triangle area, for the sector area and for forming triangle minus sector = 101; a method mark for solving for r and an accuracy mark for 29.7.
