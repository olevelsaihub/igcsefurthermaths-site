Knowledge card

Arc length, sector area and perimeter

Calculation

For a sector, arc $s = r\theta$ and area $A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$, with $\theta$ in radians. The perimeter adds both radii to the arc.

Half in the area

Trap
Forgetting the $\frac{1}{2}$ doubles the area.

Add both radii

Scores
Add both radii and the arc, not the arc alone.

3 steps to find

Which conversion comes first?

  1. Multiply by π/180
  2. Use π/3, not 60
  3. Keep π in the area

Multiply by π/180

Why
The formulae need radians, and $\pi$ radians is $180^\circ$.

Use π/3, not 60

Trap
Putting $60$ into $r\theta$ gives $360$, which is wrong.

Keep π in the area

Working
Give $6\pi$ for an exact answer, not a decimal.

5

The method

  1. Check the angle

    Make sure $\theta$ is in radians. If it is in degrees, multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$.

  2. Pick the formula

    Use $s = r\theta$ for an arc or $A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$ for an area.

  3. Add for a perimeter

    A perimeter is two radii plus the arc.

  4. Substitute and simplify

    Put in the values and keep $\pi$ if an exact answer is asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Degrees in the formula

    Convert $60^\circ$ to $\frac{\pi}{3}$ before using either formula.

  • ✗
    Missing the half

    The area formula has $\frac{1}{2}$, so $\frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$ is not $r^2\theta$.

Sector $AOB$ of a circle has centre $O$ and radius 7 cm. Angle $AOB = 1.2$ radians. (b) Calculate the perimeter of sector $AOB$.

Worked explanation

  1. Arc $AB = r\theta = 7 \times 1.2 = 8.4$ cm
  2. Perimeter $= OA + OB + \text{arc } AB = 7 + 7 + 8.4$
  3. $= 22.4$ cm
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Perimeter = 22.4 cm

How the marks are earned

A method mark for using arc = r theta and adding the two radii, and an accuracy mark for 22.4.

Calculation check

  • 7 × 1.2 = 8.4
  • 2 × 7+7 × 1.2 = 22.4
