Setting up and solving equations in r and theta from given facts about a sector, such as its area and perimeter together, or a sector rolled into a cone.

Sector problems with an unknown radius or angle

Calculation

Two facts about a sector give two equations in $r$ and $\theta$. Substitute to remove $\theta$, solve the quadratic, and reject any root that gives $\theta \ge 2\pi$.

Use rθ, not θ

Why
The perimeter gives $r\theta$ directly, so $\theta$ need not be found first.

Substitute into the area

Working
The area is $\frac{1}{2} \times r \times (r\theta)$.

Quadratic in r

Trap
Dropping the $\frac{1}{2}$ changes every coefficient.

Why

The calculation

$A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta$, $\quad s = r\theta$, $\quad P = 2r + r\theta$

Dividing area by arc length removes $\theta$: $\frac{A}{s} = \frac{r}{2}$.

  • Losing the $\frac{1}{2}$ when substituting $r\theta$ into the area
  • Keeping a root that gives $\theta \ge 2\pi$

5

The method

  1. Write each fact

    Turn the perimeter and area into equations in $r$ and $\theta$.

  2. Eliminate θ

    Use $r\theta = P - 2r$ and substitute it into $\frac{1}{2}r(r\theta)$.

  3. Solve the quadratic

    Factorise or use the formula, and keep both roots for now.

  4. Test each root

    Find $\theta$ for each root and reject any with $\theta \ge 2\pi$.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong substitution

    Replace $r\theta$ in the area formula, not $\theta$.

  • ✗
    Keeping both roots

    Check each root against the sector conditions before answering.

Sector problems with an unknown radius or angle · November 2021 · Paper 1 · Question 3 · part (whole) · 8 marks

Sector $AOB$ has centre $O$, radius $r$ cm where $r$ is an integer, and angle $AOB = \theta$ radians. The sector has area $16.8$ cm$^2$ and perimeter $16.4$ cm. Calculate (i) the value of $r$, (ii) the value of $\theta$.

Worked explanation

  1. Area: $\frac{1}{2}r^2\theta = 16.8$, so $r\theta = \frac{33.6}{r}$
  2. Perimeter: $2r + r\theta = 16.4$
  3. Substitute: $2r + \frac{33.6}{r} = 16.4 \Rightarrow 2r^2 - 16.4r + 33.6 = 0 \Rightarrow r^2 - 8.2r + 16.8 = 0$
  4. $r = \frac{8.2 \pm \sqrt{8.2^2 - 4(16.8)}}{2} = \frac{8.2 \pm \sqrt{0.04}}{2} = \frac{8.2 \pm 0.2}{2}$, so $r = 4.2$ or $r = 4$
  5. $r$ is an integer, so $r = 4$
  6. $\theta = \frac{33.6}{r^2} = \frac{33.6}{16} = 2.1$
  7. Check: perimeter $= 8 + 4(2.1) = 16.4$ and area $= \frac{1}{2}(16)(2.1) = 16.8$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

(i) $r = 4$ (ii) $\theta = 2.1$

How the marks are earned

Method marks for the area equation, the perimeter equation, eliminating theta and solving the quadratic; accuracy marks for the quadratic, for r = 4 with 4.2 rejected, and for theta = 2.1.

Calculation check

  • round((8.2+(8.2 × × 2-4 × 16.8) × × 0.5) ÷ 2,4) = 4.2
  • round((8.2-(8.2 × × 2-4 × 16.8) × × 0.5) ÷ 2,4) = 4.0
  • 33.6 ÷ 4 × × 2 = 2.1
  • 2 × 4+4 × 2.1 = 16.4
  • 0.5 × 4 × × 2 × 2.1 = 16.8
