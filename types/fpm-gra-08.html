Knowledge card

Solving an equation with your graph and a straight line

Written

To solve an equation with a graph, rearrange until one side is the plotted curve $f(x)$. Draw the straight line on the other side; the $x$-values where they meet are the roots.

1

Rearrange, then find the line

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 lines to find

Which side is the straight line?

  1. Divide every term by x²
  2. Move the constant across
  3. Read x, not y

Divide every term by x²

Why
Dividing by $x^2$ turns the constant $1$ into $\frac{1}{x^2}$.
Trap
Dividing only the $x^3$ and $x^2$ terms loses the $1$.

Move the constant across

Why
Moving the $-3$ across gives the line $y = 3$.

Read x, not y

Why
The roots are the $x$-values where the curve meets $y = 3$.
0 of 3 found

2 lines to find

Which term is the easiest to forget?

  1. Divide all four terms
  2. Keep the minus on 5/x²

Divide all four terms

Trap
Dividing only some terms gives $2x + 1 - 5 = 0$, which is wrong.

Keep the minus on 5/x²

Why
Moving $+1$ across gives $2x - \frac{5}{x^2} = -1$.
0 of 2 found
2

The calculation

$f(x) = g(x)$: the roots are the $x$-values where $y = f(x)$ meets $y = g(x)$

  • Giving the y-coordinate instead of the x-coordinate
  • Drawing a line too short to reach a root

Try itSolve $x^3 + 2 = 3x$ using $y = x^2 + \frac{2}{x}$.

  1. Divide by $x$: $x^2 + \frac{2}{x} = 3$
  2. Draw $y = 3$; the roots are where $y = x^2 + \frac{2}{x}$ meets it

5

The method

  1. Write the curve

    Name the plotted curve $f(x)$ you were given.

  2. Rearrange

    Divide, move terms or use an identity until $f(x)$ is alone on one side.

  3. Identify the line

    The other side is the straight line $y = mx + c$.

  4. Draw the line

    Use two or three points spread across the interval.

  5. Read the x-values

    Give each intersection inside the interval to the accuracy asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Forgetting to divide

    Every term must be divided, including the constants.

  • ✗
    Reading y

    The roots are the x-values, not the y-coordinates where they meet.

What a complete response includes

Method marks for a correct rearrangement to the plotted function and for drawing the correct line, then accuracy marks for each root read within an allowed range. A correct line with roots read from it scores well even if the rearrangement is not fully written out.

Solving an equation with your graph and a straight line · November 2021 · Paper 2 · Question 8 · part (c) · 5 marks

The graph of $y = 2x + \frac{3}{x^2} - 3$ has been drawn for $0.5 \leqslant x \leqslant 5$. By drawing a suitable straight line on the grid, obtain estimates, to one decimal place, of the roots of the equation $4x^3 - 10x^2 + 3 = 0$ for $0.5 \leqslant x \leqslant 5$.

Worked explanation

  1. Divide $4x^3 - 10x^2 + 3 = 0$ by $x^2$: $4x - 10 + \frac{3}{x^2} = 0$
  2. So $\frac{3}{x^2} = 10 - 4x$
  3. Add $2x - 3$ to both sides: $2x + \frac{3}{x^2} - 3 = 2x - 3 + 10 - 4x = 7 - 2x$
  4. Draw the line $y = 7 - 2x$: points $(0.5, 6)$, $(2, 3)$, $(3.5, 0)$
  5. The line crosses the curve twice in the interval, near $x = 0.63$ and $x = 2.37$
  6. Check: at $x = 0.634$ the curve gives $5.73$ and the line gives $5.73$; algebraically $4x^3 - 10x^2 + 3 = (2x + 1)(2x^2 - 6x + 3)$, whose roots in range are $\frac{3 \pm \sqrt{3}}{2} = 0.634, 2.366$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$x \approx 0.6$ and $x \approx 2.4$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for rearranging to $2x + \frac{3}{x^2} - 3 = 7 - 2x$ (identifying the line), a mark for drawing $y = 7 - 2x$ correctly on the grid, and accuracy marks for each root read from the graph within the allowed tolerance.

Calculation check

  • round(2 × 4+3 ÷ 4 × × 2-3,2) = 5.19
  • round(2 × 5+3 ÷ 5 × × 2-3,2) = 7.12
  • round(2 × 0.634+3 ÷ 0.634 × × 2-3,2) = 5.73
  • round(7-2 × 0.634,2) = 5.73
  • round((3-3 × × 0.5) ÷ 2,3) = 0.634
  • round((3+3 × × 0.5) ÷ 2,3) = 2.366
  • 4 × (-0.5) × × 3-10 × (-0.5) × × 2+3 = 0.0
