Knowledge card

Velocity and displacement by integrating

Calculation
  • $v = \int a\,dt$ and $s = \int v\,dt$: integration reverses the $s \to v \to a$ chain.
  • Every integration needs $+c$.
  • Find $c$ from a known condition (e.g. at rest when $t = 0$, or at position $(d, 0)$).
  • Do not assume $c = 0$ unless the condition shows it.
  • Substitute the required time after finding $c$.

Before you begin

  • Integrating polynomials (and simple trig or exponentials).
  • Substituting and solving linear equations.
  • The link between displacement, velocity and acceleration.
1

The method

  1. Integrate the given expression with respect to $t$.

    Why: This moves one step back along $a \to v \to s$.

  2. Add $+c$.

    Why: The constant holds the starting information.

  3. Use the given condition to find $c$.

    Why: Without it the answer is not fixed.

  4. Substitute the required time (or solve for the unknown).

    Why: This gives the final value.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Forgetting $+c$ or assuming it is zero.
  • ✗
    Differentiating instead of integrating.
  • ✗
    Fraction slips, e.g. $\int 23t\,dt = \frac{23}{2}t^2$.
  • ✗
    Using the condition at the wrong time.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from typical examiner credit patterns for this method; not measured student-error statistics.

Velocity and displacement by integrating · January 2022 · Paper 2 · Question 5 · part (c) · 4 marks

A particle $P$ is moving along the $x$-axis with velocity $v = 3t^2 - 23t + 30$ m/s at time $t$ seconds ($t \geqslant 0$). When $t = 0$, $P$ is at the point with coordinates $(d, 0)$. Given that, when $t = 8$, $P$ is at the point with coordinates $(26, 0)$, find the value of $d$.

Question restated by olevels.com from the original paper; the original is below.

View full original question and diagrams
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $x = \int(3t^2 - 23t + 30)\,dt = t^3 - \frac{23}{2}t^2 + 30t + c$
  2. At $t = 0$, $x = d$, so $c = d$.
  3. At $t = 8$, $x = 26$: $512 - \frac{23}{2}(64) + 240 + d = 26$
  4. $512 - 736 + 240 + d = 26 \Rightarrow 16 + d = 26 \Rightarrow d = 10$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$d = 10$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for integrating $v$, accuracy for the integrated terms, a method mark for using $t = 8$, $x = 26$ with the constant $d$, and accuracy for $d = 10$.

Calculation check

  • 8 × × 3 - 23 ÷ 2 × 8 × × 2 + 30 × 8 = 16.0
  • 26 - 16 = 10
