Knowledge card

Velocity and acceleration by differentiating

Calculation
  • Velocity is the rate of change of displacement: $v = \frac{ds}{dt}$.
  • Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity: $a = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2s}{dt^2}$.
  • Substitute the time after differentiating.
  • Units: displacement in m, velocity in m/s, acceleration in m/s$^2$.
  • Distance from the origin is the size of the displacement; a change in position is the difference of two displacements.

Before you begin

  • Differentiating polynomials, products and trig functions.
  • Substituting values carefully.
1

The method

  1. Decide which quantity you have ($s$ or $v$) and which you need.

    Why: Going from $s$ to $v$ to $a$ is differentiation.

  2. Differentiate (once or twice) with respect to $t$.

    Why: Each differentiation moves one step along $s \to v \to a$.

  3. Substitute the given time and state the value with units.

    Why: This is the accuracy mark.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Substituting into $v$ when $a$ is asked (or into $s$ when $v$ is asked).
  • ✗
    Differentiating the wrong function.
  • ✗
    Leaving out units or giving a decimal when an exact value is asked.
  • ✗
    Differentiating a constant term to something other than 0.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from typical examiner credit patterns for this method; not measured student-error statistics.

Velocity and acceleration by differentiating · June 2019 · Paper 2 · Question 3 · part (a) · 2 marks

A particle $P$ moves in a straight line. At time $t$ seconds, the velocity, $v$ m/s, of $P$ is given by $v = t^2 - 4t + 7$. Find the acceleration of $P$, in m/s$^2$, when $t = 3$.

Question restated by olevels.com from the original paper; the original is below.

View full original question and diagrams
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $a = \frac{dv}{dt} = 2t - 4$
  2. When $t = 3$: $a = 2(3) - 4 = 2$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$a = 2$ m/s$^2$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for differentiating the velocity, and an accuracy mark for 2.

Calculation check

  • 2 × 3 - 4 = 2
