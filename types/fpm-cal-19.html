Area between a curve and the x-axis

Calculation

The area between a curve and the x-axis is an integral, but a part below the axis gives a negative value: split at each crossing and add the sizes.

The calculation

$\text{Area} = \left|\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\right|$ for each piece between crossings

Split at each $x$-intercept, then add the sizes of the pieces.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Integrating across a root: the parts above and below cancel
  • Keeping the negative sign on an area below the axis

Try itFind the total area between $y = x^3 - x$ and the $x$-axis from $-1$ to $1$.

  1. Roots at $-1, 0, 1$: $\int_{-1}^{0}(x^3 - x)\,dx = \frac{1}{4}$ and $\int_0^1 (x^3 - x)\,dx = -\frac{1}{4}$
  2. Total area $= \frac{1}{4} + \left|-\frac{1}{4}\right| = \frac{1}{2}$

5

The method

  1. Find the crossings

    Solve $y = 0$ for the $x$-intercepts.

  2. Split the interval

    Integrate each piece on its own.

  3. Take sizes

    Pieces below the axis give negative integrals: use their size.

  4. Add

    The total area is the sum of the sizes.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    One integral across

    Parts above and below the axis cancel, giving the wrong total.

  • ✗
    Negative area

    An area is never negative: change the sign of any piece below the axis.

Area between a curve and the x-axis · November 2021 · Paper 1 · Question 10 · part (d) · 4 marks

The curve $C$ has equation $y = \frac{1}{2} + \sin 3x$, $0 \leqslant x < \frac{2\pi}{3}$. $C$ crosses the $x$-axis at $M\left(\frac{7\pi}{18}, 0\right)$ and at $N$. The finite region bounded by $C$, the $y$-axis and the part of the $x$-axis from $O$ to $N$ is shaded (part of it is above the axis, from $O$ to $M$, and part is below, from $M$ to $N$). Use algebraic integration to find, to 3 significant figures, the total area of the shaded region.

Worked explanation

  1. $N$: $\sin 3x = -\frac{1}{2}$ gives $3x = \frac{7\pi}{6}$ or $\frac{11\pi}{6}$, so $x = \frac{7\pi}{18}$ ($M$) and $x = \frac{11\pi}{18}$ ($N$).
  2. $\int\left(\frac{1}{2} + \sin 3x\right)dx = \frac{x}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\cos 3x$
  3. Above the axis: $\left[\frac{x}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\cos 3x\right]_0^{\frac{7\pi}{18}} = \left(\frac{7\pi}{36} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\right) - \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right) = 1.2329\ldots$
  4. Below the axis: $\left[\frac{x}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\cos 3x\right]_{\frac{7\pi}{18}}^{\frac{11\pi}{18}} = \left(\frac{11\pi}{36} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\right) - \left(\frac{7\pi}{36} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\right) = \frac{\pi}{9} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = -0.2283\ldots$
  5. Total area $= 1.2329\ldots + 0.2283\ldots = 1.4612\ldots$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Total area $\approx 1.46$ (3 s.f.)

How the marks are earned

A method mark for integrating $\frac{1}{2} + \sin 3x$, a method mark for using the limits $0$, $\frac{7\pi}{18}$ and $\frac{11\pi}{18}$ with the region split at $M$, a method mark for adding the size of the negative part, and accuracy for 1.46.

Calculation check

  • round(7 × 3.141592653589793 ÷ 36 + 3 × × 0.5 ÷ 6 + 1 ÷ 3, 4) = 1.2329
  • round(3.141592653589793 ÷ 9 - 3 × × 0.5 ÷ 3, 4) = -0.2283
  • round(7 × 3.141592653589793 ÷ 36 + 3 × × 0.5 ÷ 6 + 1 ÷ 3 - (3.141592653589793 ÷ 9 - 3 × × 0.5 ÷ 3), 2) = 1.46
