Equation of a perpendicular line (including perpendicular bisectors)

Calculation

A perpendicular line has gradient $-\frac{1}{m}$ and passes through a given point. A perpendicular bisector also passes through the midpoint.

The calculation

$m_\perp = -\frac{1}{m}$, and $y - y_1 = m_\perp(x - x_1)$

Perpendicular bisector: through the midpoint, at right angles to the segment

  • Using $-m$ or $\frac{1}{m}$ instead of $-\frac{1}{m}$
  • Using an end point instead of the midpoint

Try itFind the perpendicular bisector of $A(1, 1)$ and $B(5, 3)$ in the form $ax + by + c = 0$.

  1. Midpoint $(3, 2)$; gradient $AB = \frac{1}{2}$, so $m_\perp = -2$
  2. $y - 2 = -2(x - 3)$, so $2x + y - 8 = 0$

5

The method

  1. Find the gradient

    Read it from the line, or rearrange to $y = mx + c$.

  2. Take the negative reciprocal

    Use $m_\perp = -\frac{1}{m}$.

  3. Use the point

    Write $y - y_1 = m_\perp(x - x_1)$ with the given point.

  4. Clear and rearrange

    Multiply out to the form asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    No minus sign

    The perpendicular to $\frac{1}{3}$ is $-3$, not $3$.

  • ✗
    Wrong point

    Use the point the question gives.

What a complete response includes

Method marks are spread across the stages: the original gradient, the negative reciprocal, finding the point (midpoint or ratio point) and forming the line, followed by an accuracy mark for the final equation in the required or printed form.

Equation of a perpendicular line (including perpendicular bisectors) · January 2022 · Paper 1 · Question 10 · part (b) · 4 marks

The equation of the line $L_1$ is $y - 2x - 6 = 0$. The point $P$ with coordinates $(2, a)$ lies on $L_1$. The line $L_2$ is perpendicular to $L_1$ and passes through $P$. (b) Show that an equation of $L_2$ is $x + 2y - 22 = 0$.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. From part (a): $a - 2(2) - 6 = 0$, so $a = 10$ and $P$ is $(2, 10)$.
  2. $L_1$: $y = 2x + 6$, so its gradient is $2$.
  3. Gradient of $L_2 = -\frac{1}{2}$
  4. $y - 10 = -\frac{1}{2}(x - 2)$
  5. $2y - 20 = -x + 2$
  6. $x + 2y - 22 = 0$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$x + 2y - 22 = 0$, as required.

How the marks are earned

A method mark for the gradient of $L_1$, a method mark for the negative reciprocal $-\frac{1}{2}$, a method mark for a line through $(2, 10)$ with that gradient, and an accuracy mark for reaching $x + 2y - 22 = 0$ with no errors.

Calculation check

  • 10-2 × 2-6 = 0
  • 2+2 × 10-22 = 0
  • 2 × (-1 ÷ 2) = -1
