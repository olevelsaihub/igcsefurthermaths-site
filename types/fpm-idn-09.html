Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Further Pure Mathematics · 4PM1

Two constants from factor and remainder information

Forming two equations in two unknown coefficients from factor and remainder information (sometimes about f'(x) or after integrating) and solving them simultaneously.

Type 12 3 Identities and inequalities FPM-IDN-09

★★★★☆ · 11 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

A polynomial with two unknown constants and two conditions, e.g. '(x + 2) is a factor of f(x) and when f(x) is divided by (x + 3) the remainder is 21', followed by 'show that a = 2 and find the value of b' or 'find the value of p and the value of q'.

Try it$f(x) = x^3 + bx + c$ has factor $(x - 2)$ and leaves remainder $3$ when divided by $(x + 1)$. Find $b$ and $c$.

  1. $f(2) = 0$: $2b + c = -8$; $f(-1) = 3$: $-b + c = 4$
  2. Solving: $b = -4$ and $c = 0$

5

The method

  1. Write each condition

    A factor gives f(a) = 0; a remainder gives f(a) = R.

  2. Substitute

    Put each value into f(x) and simplify.

  3. Solve

    Eliminate one constant from the two equations.

  4. Check

    Substitute into one original condition.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Factor and remainder swapped

    A factor is = 0; a remainder is = R.

  • ✗
    Sign with negatives

    Write (−2)³ = −8 before combining.

What a complete response includes

A method mark for each equation formed by substitution, a method mark for solving the pair simultaneously, and accuracy marks for each constant (the first often as a shown result). The f' versions add marks for differentiating or integrating correctly.

Two constants from factor and remainder information · January 2022 · Paper 1 · Question 5 · part (a) · 5 marks

$f(x)=ax^3+5bx^2+8ax-4b$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers. Given that $(x+2)$ is a factor of $f(x)$ and that when $f(x)$ is divided by $(x+3)$ the remainder is $21$, show that $a=2$ and find the value of $b$.

Worked explanation

  1. Factor $(x+2)$: $f(-2)=0$
  2. $a(-8)+5b(4)+8a(-2)-4b=0 \Rightarrow -24a+16b=0 \Rightarrow 2b=3a$
  3. Remainder on $(x+3)$: $f(-3)=21$
  4. $a(-27)+5b(9)+8a(-3)-4b=21 \Rightarrow -51a+41b=21$
  5. From the first equation $b=\frac{3a}{2}$. Substitute: $-51a+\frac{123a}{2}=21 \Rightarrow \frac{21a}{2}=21 \Rightarrow a=2$, as required.
  6. $b=\frac{3(2)}{2}=3$
  7. Check: $f(x)=2x^3+15x^2+16x-12$, and $f(-2)=-16+60-32-12=0$.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$a=2$, $b=3$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for $f(-2)=0$, a method mark for $f(-3)=21$, a method mark for solving the two equations together, an accuracy mark for $a=2$ obtained from the equations, and an accuracy mark for $b=3$.

Calculation check

  • -24 × 2+16 × 3 = 0
  • -51 × 2+41 × 3 = 21
  • 2 × (-2) × × 3+15 × (-2) × × 2+16 × (-2)-12 = 0
  • 2 × (-3) × × 3+15 × (-3) × × 2+16 × (-3)-12 = 21
