Finding the angle between two slanted faces of a solid by dropping perpendiculars from each face onto their common edge and using the cosine rule.

Knowledge card

Angle between two sloping faces with the cosine rule

Calculation

The angle between two sloping faces is found from perpendiculars to their common edge, then the cosine rule. The angle between faces of a pyramid is often obtuse.

1

Angle between two faces

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

4 lines to find

Which area gives the perpendicular?

  1. Slant height from M
  2. Area with base AB
  3. VA from the half diagonal
  4. Same length from D

Slant height from M

Why
$M$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and $OM = 3$ is half the side.

Area with base AB

Why
Area is $\frac{1}{2} \times$ base $\times$ slant height.

VA from the half diagonal

Trap
Use the half diagonal $3\sqrt{2}$, not the side $3$.

Same length from D

Why
By symmetry the perpendicular from $D$ meets $VA$ at the same point.
0 of 4 found

4 lines to find

Which sides go into the cosine rule?

  1. Equal perpendiculars
  2. Diagonal of the base
  3. Cosine rule at X
  4. Obtuse angle

Equal perpendiculars

Why
Symmetry makes $BX = DX$, both meeting $VA$ at $X$.

Diagonal of the base

Working
$BD$ is the base diagonal, $6\sqrt{2}$.

Cosine rule at X

Working
$\cos BXD = \frac{BX^2 + DX^2 - BD^2}{2 \times BX \times DX}$

Obtuse angle

Scores
The cosine is negative, so the angle is obtuse.

2

The calculation

$\cos\theta = \frac{p^2 + q^2 - d^2}{2pq}$, where $p$ and $q$ are the perpendiculars and $d$ joins their feet

A negative cosine gives an obtuse angle.

  • Using a slant edge in place of the perpendicular to the common edge
  • Giving the acute angle when the obtuse one is asked

5

The method

  1. Find the common edge

    Identify the edge where the two faces meet: here VA.

  2. Drop perpendiculars

    From B and D, drop perpendiculars to VA, meeting it at one point X.

  3. Find the three lengths

    Find BX (equal to DX) and the base diagonal BD, using the area method and Pythagoras.

  4. Use the cosine rule

    Use $\cos BXD = \frac{BX^2 + DX^2 - BD^2}{2\,BX\,DX}$ and give the angle asked for.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong edge

    Measure across the common edge, not a base edge.

  • ✗
    Slant edge instead of perpendicular

    Use the perpendicular to VA, not the slant edge itself.

Angle between two sloping faces with the cosine rule · June 2025 · Paper 1 · Question 8 · part (e) · 3 marks

A right pyramid has vertex $V$ and square base $ABCD$ of side 12 cm. The angle between plane $VAB$ and the base is $30^\circ$. Earlier parts give: height $2\sqrt{3}$ cm, $VA = 2\sqrt{21}$ cm, area of triangle $VAD = 24\sqrt{3}$ cm$^2$ and the perpendicular from $D$ to $VA$ $= \frac{24}{\sqrt{7}}$ cm. (e) Find, in degrees to one decimal place, the size of the obtuse angle between the plane $VAB$ and the plane $VAD$.

Worked explanation

  1. Earlier results: $h = 6\tan 30^\circ = 2\sqrt{3}$; $VA^2 = (2\sqrt{3})^2 + (6\sqrt{2})^2 = 12 + 72 = 84$, so $VA = 2\sqrt{21}$; slant height of a face $= \sqrt{12 + 36} = 4\sqrt{3}$, area $VAD = \frac{1}{2}(12)(4\sqrt{3}) = 24\sqrt{3}$.
  2. Perpendicular from $D$ to $VA$: $DX = \frac{2 \times 24\sqrt{3}}{2\sqrt{21}} = \frac{24}{\sqrt{7}}$; by symmetry the perpendicular from $B$ meets $VA$ at the same point $X$, with $BX = \frac{24}{\sqrt{7}}$.
  3. The angle between the planes is $\angle BXD$. $BD = 12\sqrt{2}$, so $BD^2 = 288$, and $BX^2 = DX^2 = \frac{576}{7}$.
  4. $\cos\angle BXD = \frac{\frac{576}{7} + \frac{576}{7} - 288}{2 \times \frac{576}{7}} = \frac{164.57 - 288}{164.57} = -0.75$
  5. $\angle BXD = 138.6^\circ$ (1 d.p.)
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$138.6^\circ$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for using the two equal perpendiculars to VA, a method mark for the cosine rule with BD = 12 root 2, and the accuracy mark for 138.6 degrees.
