Knowledge card

Unit vectors: same direction, length one

Calculation

A unit vector has length $1$. Divide a vector by its own magnitude to get the unit vector in the same direction: $\hat{\mathbf{v}}=\frac{\mathbf{v}}{|\mathbf{v}|}$.

5

The method

  1. Find the vector

    Get the vector in component form, subtracting position vectors if needed.

  2. Find its length

    Use $|\mathbf{v}|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$.

  3. Divide each part

    Divide both the $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$ parts by $|\mathbf{v}|$.

  4. Simplify

    Cancel, or rationalise if the question asks.

  5. Use a given unit vector

    If one is given, compare components to find unknowns.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong length

    Use the length of the displacement, not of a position vector.

  • ✗
    Missing root

    Divide by the length, not its square.

What a complete response includes

Short parts give a method mark for dividing the right vector by its magnitude and an accuracy mark for the answer. Longer parts first give method and accuracy marks for finding the vector and its magnitude. Reverse versions give marks for equating components and solving for each constant.

Unit vectors: same direction, length one · January 2022 · Paper 1 · Regional (R) · Question 6 · part (b) · 4 marks

The points $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ are the vertices of a quadrilateral $PQRS$ such that $\overrightarrow{PQ}=2\mathbf{i}+3\mathbf{j}$, $\overrightarrow{PR}=-\mathbf{i}+18\mathbf{j}$ and $\overrightarrow{PS}=-3\mathbf{i}+15\mathbf{j}$. (b) Find a unit vector parallel to $\overrightarrow{QS}$. (4 marks)

Worked explanation

  1. $\overrightarrow{QS}=\overrightarrow{PS}-\overrightarrow{PQ}=(-3\mathbf{i}+15\mathbf{j})-(2\mathbf{i}+3\mathbf{j})=-5\mathbf{i}+12\mathbf{j}$
  2. $|\overrightarrow{QS}|=\sqrt{(-5)^2+12^2}=\sqrt{169}=13$
  3. Unit vector $=\dfrac{-5\mathbf{i}+12\mathbf{j}}{13}=-\dfrac{5}{13}\mathbf{i}+\dfrac{12}{13}\mathbf{j}$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$\frac{1}{13}(-5\mathbf{i}+12\mathbf{j})$

How the marks are earned

A method mark and accuracy mark for $\overrightarrow{QS}=-5\mathbf{i}+12\mathbf{j}$, a method mark for its magnitude 13, and an accuracy mark for dividing to get the unit vector. (The vector in the opposite direction, $\frac{1}{13}(5\mathbf{i}-12\mathbf{j})$, is also parallel and would normally be accepted.)

Calculation check

  • -3-2 = -5
  • 15-3 = 12
  • ((-5) × × 2+12 × × 2) × × 0.5 = 13.0
  • round((-5 ÷ 13) × × 2+(12 ÷ 13) × × 2,10) = 1.0
