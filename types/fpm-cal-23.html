Knowledge card

Volumes of revolution between two boundaries

Calculation

For a region between an outer and an inner graph, the volume is $\pi\int (y_1^2 - y_2^2)\,dx$: subtract the squares, never the difference squared.

The calculation

$V = \pi\int_a^b \left(y_1^2 - y_2^2\right)dx$, where $y_1$ is the outer graph

Outer is the boundary further from the axis. Add the volumes of separate pieces when the boundary changes.

  • Squaring the difference: $(y_1 - y_2)^2$ is the wrong formula
  • Taking the inner graph as outer, which gives a negative answer

Try itFind the volume when the region between $y = 2$ and $y = x$ from $x = 0$ to $x = 2$ is rotated about the $x$-axis.

  1. $V = \pi\int_0^2 (2^2 - x^2)\,dx = \pi\int_0^2 (4 - x^2)\,dx$
  2. $= \pi\left[4x - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^2 = \frac{16\pi}{3}$

5

The method

  1. Find the intersections

    Solve the two equations together for the limits.

  2. Name the outer graph

    The outer graph is further from the x-axis between the limits.

  3. Square each, then subtract

    Use $y_1^2 - y_2^2$.

  4. Integrate, then multiply by π

    Keep the answer exact.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Squaring the difference

    $(y_1 - y_2)^2$ is the wrong formula.

  • ✗
    Half the region

    If the region is symmetric, use the full interval or double the half.

Volumes of revolution between two boundaries · November 2021 · Paper 2 · Question 2 · part (whole) · 6 marks

The finite region enclosed by the curve with equation $y = 4 - x^2$ and the line with equation $y = x + 2$ is rotated through $360°$ about the $x$-axis. Use algebraic integration to find the exact volume of the solid formed.

Worked explanation

  1. Intersections: $4 - x^2 = x + 2 \Rightarrow x^2 + x - 2 = 0 \Rightarrow (x + 2)(x - 1) = 0$, so $x = -2$ or $x = 1$.
  2. For $-2 < x < 1$ the curve is above the line, and both are above the $x$-axis.
  3. $V = \pi\int_{-2}^{1}\left[(4 - x^2)^2 - (x + 2)^2\right]dx = \pi\int_{-2}^{1}(x^4 - 9x^2 - 4x + 12)\,dx$
  4. $= \pi\left[\frac{x^5}{5} - 3x^3 - 2x^2 + 12x\right]_{-2}^{1}$
  5. $= \pi\left[\left(\frac{1}{5} - 3 - 2 + 12\right) - \left(-\frac{32}{5} + 24 - 8 - 24\right)\right] = \pi\left[\frac{36}{5} + \frac{72}{5}\right] = \frac{108\pi}{5}$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$V = \frac{108\pi}{5}$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for solving to find the limits, a method mark for $\pi\int (y_1^2 - y_2^2)\,dx$ with correct squares, a method mark for integrating, a method mark for substituting the limits, and accuracy for $\frac{108\pi}{5}$.

Calculation check

  • (1 ÷ 5 - 3 - 2 + 12) - ((-2) × × 5 ÷ 5 - 3 × (-2) × × 3 - 2 × (-2) × × 2 + 12 × (-2)) = 21.6
  • 108 ÷ 5 = 21.6
