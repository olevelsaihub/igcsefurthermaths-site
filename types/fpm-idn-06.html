5

The method

  1. Draw each line

    Plot the boundary lines with their intercepts.

  2. Test each side

    Use a point not on the line to choose the wanted side.

  3. Find the overlap

    R is on the wanted side of every line.

  4. Shade and label

    Shade R or the unwanted parts as asked, then label it R.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong side

    Test a point; do not guess from the picture.

  • ✗
    Test point on the line

    Choose a point not on the boundary, such as (1, 0).

What a complete response includes

Usually one or two B marks that need the whole region correct: every boundary in the right place and on the right side, with R identified. When writing down inequalities, there is typically one mark per correct inequality.

Shading or describing a region · June 2023 · Paper 2 · Question 5 · part (b) · 1 mark

Show, by shading, the region $R$ defined by the inequalities $y\leqslant 2x+5$, $4y\geqslant x-8$, $5y+3x\leqslant 30$ (the three lines from part (a)).

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Test the origin $(0,0)$ in each inequality.
  2. $y\leqslant 2x+5$: $0\leqslant 5$, true, so $R$ is on the origin side of line (i), which is below/right of it.
  3. $4y\geqslant x-8$: $0\geqslant -8$, true, so $R$ is on the origin side of line (ii), which is above it.
  4. $5y+3x\leqslant 30$: $0\leqslant 30$, true, so $R$ is on the origin side of line (iii), which is below it.
  5. The region on the origin side of all three lines is the triangle with corners where the lines meet: $(-4,-3)$, about $(0.38,5.77)$ and about $(9.41,0.35)$.
  6. Shade this triangle and label it R.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The triangle containing the origin, bounded by the three lines, with vertices $(-4,-3)$, $\left(\frac{5}{13},\frac{75}{13}\right)$ and $\left(\frac{160}{17},\frac{6}{17}\right)$, shaded and labelled $R$.

How the marks are earned

A single mark for shading the correct triangle; every boundary must be the correct line and the correct side.

Calculation check

  • 2 × 0+5 = 5
  • 0-8 = -8
  • 5 × 0+3 × 0 = 0
  • 4 × (-3)-(-4-8) = 0
  • round(5 × (75 ÷ 13)+3 × (5 ÷ 13), 9) = 30
