The calculation

$(1+x)^n = 1 + nx + \frac{n(n-1)}{2!}x^2 + \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{3!}x^3 + \dots$

For $(1+bx)^n$ put $bx$ in place of $x$ in each term.

The method

  1. Set up

    Name $n$ and $b$: here $n = -2$, $b = -2$.

  2. Write the terms

    Use brackets: $(bx)$, $(bx)^2$, $(bx)^3$.

  3. Find each coefficient

    Use $\frac{n(n-1)}{2}$ and $\frac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{6}$.

  4. Simplify

    Keep signs and write the powers in order.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Squared coefficient

    $(-2x)^2 = 4x^2$, not $-2x^2$.

  • ✗
    Missing factorial

    Divide by $2!$, then $3!$.

(a) Expand $\sqrt{1 - 4x}$ in ascending powers of $x$ up to and including the term in $x^3$, giving each coefficient as an integer.

Worked explanation

  1. $\sqrt{1 - 4x} = (1 - 4x)^{\frac{1}{2}}$, with $n = \frac{1}{2}$ and the term $(-4x)$
  2. $= 1 + \frac{1}{2}(-4x) + \frac{\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)}{2}(-4x)^2 + \frac{\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(-\frac{3}{2}\right)}{6}(-4x)^3 + \dots$
  3. $= 1 - 2x + \left(-\frac{1}{8}\right)(16x^2) + \left(\frac{1}{16}\right)(-64x^3) + \dots$
  4. $= 1 - 2x - 2x^2 - 4x^3 + \dots$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$\sqrt{1 - 4x} = 1 - 2x - 2x^2 - 4x^3 + \dots$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for the correct binomial structure with n = 1/2 and -4x, an accuracy mark for 1 - 2x, and further accuracy for the x^2 and x^3 terms -2x^2 and -4x^3.

Calculation check

  • (1 ÷ 2) × (-1 ÷ 2) ÷ 2 × 16 = -2
  • (1 ÷ 2) × (-1 ÷ 2) × (-3 ÷ 2) ÷ 6 × (-64) = -4
  • 1-2 × 0.06 = 0.88
