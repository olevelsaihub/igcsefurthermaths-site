Knowledge card

Writing down the asymptotes of a rational curve

Calculation

The vertical asymptote of $y = \frac{ax + b}{cx + d}$ comes from the zero denominator; the horizontal asymptote is $y = \frac{a}{c}$, the ratio of the $x$-coefficients.

1

Which asymptote is which

Decide first, then tap the ? to check.

3 lines to find

Which step finds each line?

  1. Denominator is zero
  2. Ratio of x-coefficients
  3. Write as an equation

Denominator is zero

Why
Division by zero is impossible, so the curve cannot reach $x = 3$.
Trap
Writing $x = -3$ flips the sign of the $-3$.

Ratio of x-coefficients

Working
For large $x$, $y \to \frac{2}{1} = 2$.

Write as an equation

Why
A bare number is not a line: write $x = 3$ and $y = 2$.
0 of 3 found

2 lines to find

Which number gives each asymptote?

  1. Read n with its sign
  2. k gives the horizontal line

Read n with its sign

Trap
$x + 2$ means $n = 2$, giving $x = -2$, not $x = 2$.

k gives the horizontal line

Why
As $x$ grows, $\frac{-3}{x + 2}$ shrinks to $0$, leaving $y = 5$.
0 of 2 found
2

The calculation

$y = \frac{ax + b}{cx + d}$: vertical $x = -\frac{d}{c}$, horizontal $y = \frac{a}{c}$

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Swapped lines: vertical is $x = \ldots$, horizontal is $y = \ldots$
  • A bare number: write $y = 2$, not $2$

Try itWrite down the asymptotes of $y = \frac{3x - 1}{x + 4}$.

  1. Vertical: $x + 4 = 0$, so $x = -4$
  2. Horizontal: $y = \frac{3}{1} = 3$

5

The method

  1. Vertical: zero denominator

    Set $cx + d = 0$ and solve for $x$.

  2. Horizontal: ratio

    Divide the $x$-coefficient on top by the one below: $y = \frac{a}{c}$.

  3. Write equations

    Give each as $x = \ldots$ or $y = \ldots$.

  4. Check

    The vertical asymptote is the value excluded from the domain.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Swapped lines

    Vertical is $x = \ldots$; horizontal is $y = \ldots$.

  • ✗
    Constant ratio

    $\frac{b}{d}$ is the $y$-intercept, not the horizontal asymptote.

Writing down the asymptotes of a rational curve · November 2021 · Paper 2 · Question 3 · part (d) · 1 mark

The curve has equation $y = \frac{a - bx}{cx - d}$, $x \neq \frac{d}{c}$, where $a$, $c$ and $d$ are prime numbers and $b$ is an integer. Earlier parts give $c = 2$, $d = 3$ (from the asymptote $x = \frac{3}{2}$) and $a = 5$, $b = 4$ (from the intercept $\left(\frac{5}{4}, 0\right)$), so $y = \frac{5 - 4x}{2x - 3}$. Find an equation of the asymptote to the curve that is parallel to the $x$-axis.

Worked explanation

  1. The curve is $y = \frac{5 - 4x}{2x - 3} = \frac{-4x + 5}{2x - 3}$
  2. Divide top and bottom by $x$: $y = \frac{-4 + \frac{5}{x}}{2 - \frac{3}{x}}$
  3. As $x$ becomes very large, $\frac{5}{x} \to 0$ and $\frac{3}{x} \to 0$, so $y \to \frac{-4}{2} = -2$
  4. Check with a large value: $x = 1000$ gives $y = \frac{5 - 4000}{2000 - 3} = -2.0005\ldots$, close to $-2$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$y = -2$

How the marks are earned

A single mark for the equation $y = -2$ (it follows through from the values of $b$ and $c$ found earlier).

Calculation check

  • -4 ÷ 2 = -2.0
  • round((5-4 × 1000) ÷ (2 × 1000-3),4) = -2.0005
  • 2 × (3 ÷ 2)-3 = 0.0
