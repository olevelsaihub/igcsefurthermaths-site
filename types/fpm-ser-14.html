Knowledge card

Finding n in a geometric series with logarithms

Calculation

To find $n$ when $r^n$ is unknown, isolate $r^n$ and take logs. If $0 < r < 1$, dividing by $\log r$ reverses the inequality.

The calculation

$r^n = k \Rightarrow n = \frac{\log k}{\log r}$

If $0 < r < 1$ then $\log r < 0$: the inequality sign reverses.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Not reversing the sign when dividing by $\log r < 0$
  • Rounding down for a least $n$

Try itFind the least $n$ with $2^n > 100$.

  1. $n > \frac{\log 100}{\log 2} \approx 6.64$
  2. Least $n = 7$, since $2^7 = 128$.

5

The method

  1. Write the sum

    Use $a(1-r^n)/(1-r)$ with the values.

  2. Isolate rⁿ

    Get the power on its own.

  3. Take logs

    Bring $n$ down with $\log r^n = n\log r$.

  4. Divide and round

    Reverse the sign if $\log r < 0$, then round up.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Sign flip

    $\log r < 0$ reverses the inequality.

  • ✗
    Rounding

    A least $n$ rounds up.

A geometric series $G$ has first term $320$ and common ratio $\frac{1}{4}$. The sum to $n$ terms of $G$ is $S_n$. (d) Find, using logarithms, the least value of $n$ such that $S_n > 426.6$

Worked explanation

  1. $S_n = \frac{320\left(1 - \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^n\right)}{1 - \frac{1}{4}} = \frac{1280}{3}\left(1 - \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^n\right)$
  2. $\frac{1280}{3}\left(1 - \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^n\right) > 426.6$
  3. $1 - \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^n > \frac{3 \times 426.6}{1280} = \frac{1279.8}{1280}$
  4. $\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^n < \frac{0.2}{1280} = \frac{1}{6400}$
  5. $n \log 0.25 < \log \frac{1}{6400}$
  6. $\log 0.25 < 0$, so dividing reverses the sign: $n > \frac{\log(1/6400)}{\log 0.25} = 6.32\ldots$
  7. Least $n = 7$
  8. Check: $S_6 = \frac{1280}{3}\left(1 - \frac{1}{4096}\right) \approx 426.56 < 426.6$; $S_7 \approx 426.64 > 426.6$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$n = 7$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for the sum formula with a = 320 and r = 1/4 set against 426.6, a method mark for rearranging to (1/4)^n < 1/6400, a method mark for taking logs and dividing correctly (with the sign reversed), and an accuracy mark for n = 7.

Calculation check

  • 0.2 ÷ 1280 = 0.00015625
  • round(1280 ÷ 3 × (1-0.25 × × 6),3) = 426.562
  • round(1280 ÷ 3 × (1-0.25 × × 7),3) = 426.641
  • 4 × × 6 = 4096
  • 4 × × 7 = 16384
