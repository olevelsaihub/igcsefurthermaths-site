Finding where a curve has a given gradient

Setting dy/dx equal to a known gradient (from a parallel line or a normal) to find the points, then using them for tangent constants, lines or lengths.

Type 25 9 Calculus FPM-CAL-06

★★☆☆☆ · 7 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

The question gives a gradient instead of a point: 'the tangent at $P$ is parallel to the tangent at $A$', 'the line $y = x + k$ is a tangent to $C$', or 'the gradient of $C$ is $\frac{13}{100}$ at $E$ and $F$'.

Knowledge card

Finding where a curve has a given gradient

Calculation

To find where a curve has a given gradient, set $\frac{dy}{dx}$ equal to that gradient and solve for $x$. Each root gives a point, and the curve gives its $y$.

A parallel tangent has the same gradient as the line.

  1. Parallel: same gradient
  2. Both square roots
  3. Read y from the curve

Parallel: same gradient

Why
The line $y = 9x + 1$ has gradient $9$.

Both square roots

Trap
Taking only $x = 2$ misses the second point.

Read y from the curve

Working
$2^3 - 3(2) = 2$ and $(-2)^3 - 3(-2) = -2$.

3 steps to find

Set $\frac{dy}{dx}$ equal to $-1$ and solve the equation.

  1. Derivative of 4/(x+1)
  2. Two solutions
  3. y from the curve

Derivative of 4/(x+1)

Working
$\frac{d}{dx}\frac{4}{x+1} = -\frac{4}{(x+1)^2}$

Two solutions

Trap
Stopping at $x = 1$ misses the second point.

y from the curve

Working
At $x = 1$: $y = 4 \div 2 = 2$.

The calculation

$\frac{dy}{dx} = m$ at each $x$ that solves it, then $y = f(x)$ at that $x$

  • Taking only the positive square root
  • Keeping a point that is already given, such as the point A

Try itFind the tangent to $y = x^2 + 4x$ that is parallel to $y = 2x$.

  1. $2x + 4 = 2$ gives $x = -1$, so $y = 1 - 4 = -3$
  2. $y + 3 = 2(x + 1)$, so $2x - y - 1 = 0$

5

The method

  1. Differentiate

    Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ as a function of $x$.

  2. Set the gradient

    Write $\frac{dy}{dx} = m$ for the required gradient $m$.

  3. Solve for x

    Solve for every root and reject any that break a condition.

  4. Find y and finish

    Substitute each $x$ into the curve, then write the line or value asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Missing a root

    A square gives two values: both $\pm$ roots count.

  • ✗
    y from the derivative

    Put $x$ into the curve, not into $\frac{dy}{dx}$.

Finding where a curve has a given gradient · June 2025 · Paper 2 · Question 10 · part (e) · 7 marks

The curve $C$ has equation $y = \frac{x+7}{2x-3}$, $x \neq \frac{3}{2}$, and passes through $A\left(-\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{13}{8}\right)$, where the gradient is $-\frac{17}{16}$. $C$ also passes through the point $P$. Given that the tangent to $C$ at $P$ is parallel to the tangent to $C$ at $A$, find an equation of the tangent to $C$ at $P$ in the form $ax + by + c = 0$ where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers.

Worked explanation

  1. From part (d), $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{-17}{(2x-3)^2}$.
  2. Parallel to the tangent at $A$: $\frac{-17}{(2x-3)^2} = -\frac{17}{16} \Rightarrow (2x-3)^2 = 16$.
  3. $2x - 3 = 4$ or $2x - 3 = -4$, so $x = \frac{7}{2}$ or $x = -\frac{1}{2}$. Since $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ is the point $A$, at $P$ we have $x = \frac{7}{2}$.
  4. $y = \frac{\frac{7}{2} + 7}{7 - 3} = \frac{21/2}{4} = \frac{21}{8}$, so $P\left(\frac{7}{2}, \frac{21}{8}\right)$.
  5. Tangent: $y - \frac{21}{8} = -\frac{17}{16}\left(x - \frac{7}{2}\right)$.
  6. Multiply by 32: $32y - 84 = -34x + 119$, so $34x + 32y - 203 = 0$.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$34x + 32y - 203 = 0$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for setting the derivative equal to $-\frac{17}{16}$, a method mark for solving $(2x-3)^2 = 16$, accuracy for $x = \frac{7}{2}$ (rejecting $-\frac{1}{2}$), accuracy for $y = \frac{21}{8}$, a method mark for the line through $P$ with gradient $-\frac{17}{16}$, and accuracy for the integer form (any non-zero multiple is equivalent).

Calculation check

  • (2 × 3.5-3) × × 2 = 16.0
  • (3.5+7) ÷ (2 × 3.5-3) = 2.625
  • 21 ÷ 8 = 2.625
  • 34 × 3.5 + 32 × 2.625 - 203 = 0.0
