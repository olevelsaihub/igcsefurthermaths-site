Knowledge card

Small changes and percentage changes

Calculation

For a small change, $\delta y \approx \frac{dy}{dx}\,\delta x$. Divide by the original value and multiply by $100$ to get a percentage change.

The calculation

$\delta y \approx \frac{dy}{dx}\,\delta x$, and percentage change $\approx \frac{\delta y}{y} \times 100\%$

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Using $\delta x = 3$ for a 3% increase instead of $0.03x$
  • Forgetting to divide by y for a percentage

Try itGiven $y = x^2$, estimate the percentage change in $y$ when $x = 4$ increases by $2\%$.

  1. $\delta x = 0.02 \times 4 = 0.08$ and $\frac{dy}{dx} = 8$, so $\delta y \approx 0.64$
  2. Percentage $\approx \frac{0.64}{16} \times 100 = 4\%$

The volume of a sphere is $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$. The radius increases by $1\%$. Use calculus to estimate the percentage increase in $V$. (3 marks)

5

The method

  1. Differentiate

    Find $\frac{dV}{dr}$ with respect to the changing variable.

  2. Write the small change

    A 1% change in $r$ is $\delta r = 0.01r$, not $0.01$.

  3. Use the approximation

    Use $\delta V \approx \frac{dV}{dr}\,\delta r$.

  4. Make it a percentage

    Divide by $V$ and multiply by $100$.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    δr as a number

    A percentage of $r$ depends on $r$: $\delta r = 0.01r$.

  • ✗
    Not dividing by V

    The percentage is relative to the original $V$.

Small changes and percentage changes · November 2021 · Paper 2 · Question 5 · part (whole) · 6 marks

$y$ and $x$ vary so that $y = xe^{-2x}$. Given that the value of $x$ increases by 3%, use calculus to find, in terms of $x$, an estimate for the percentage change in $y$. Give your answer in the form $a(b - cx)$ where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers.

View full original question and diagrams

Your answer

Write your answer first. Feedback and progress stay on this device.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $\frac{dy}{dx} = e^{-2x} + x(-2e^{-2x}) = e^{-2x}(1 - 2x)$
  2. A 3% increase: $\delta x = 0.03x$.
  3. $\delta y \approx \frac{dy}{dx}\,\delta x = e^{-2x}(1 - 2x)(0.03x)$
  4. Percentage change $\approx \frac{\delta y}{y} \times 100 = \frac{0.03x\,e^{-2x}(1 - 2x)}{x e^{-2x}} \times 100$
  5. $= 3(1 - 2x)$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Percentage change in $y \approx 3(1 - 2x)\%$ (so $a = 3$, $b = 1$, $c = 2$)

How the marks are earned

A method mark for the product rule, accuracy for $\frac{dy}{dx}$, a method mark for $\delta x = 0.03x$ in $\delta y \approx \frac{dy}{dx}\delta x$, a method mark for dividing by $y$ and multiplying by 100, and accuracy for $3(1 - 2x)$. Check at $x = 0.3$: $3(1 - 0.6) = 1.2$.

Calculation check

  • 0.03 × (1-2 × 0.3) × 100 = 1.2
  • 3 × (1-2 × 0.3) = 1.2
