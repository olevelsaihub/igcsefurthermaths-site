Turning sin and cos equations into tan

Rearranging an equation with sine and cosine of the same angle (possibly after expanding compound angles) into tan = k, then solving.

Type 14 10 Trigonometry FPM-TRI-25

★★★☆☆ · 8 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

An equation with sine and cosine of the same angle and no other terms, e.g. $\sin(2\theta - 20)^\circ - 3\cos(2\theta - 20)^\circ = 0$, or $\cos(x - 30)^\circ = \sin(x + 30)^\circ$ after expanding.

Turning sin and cos equations into tan

Calculation

Expand any compound angle, collect the $\sin$ and $\cos$ terms on opposite sides, then divide to get $\tan x = k$. Tangent repeats every $180^\circ$.

The calculation

$\tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, so $a\sin u = b\cos u$ gives $\tan u = \frac{b}{a}$

Tangent repeats every $180^\circ$, so add $180^\circ n$ to the principal value.

  • Inverting the ratio: $\tan u = \frac{a}{b}$ is wrong
  • Missing the solution a half-turn later

Try itSolve $\sin x = 2\cos x$ for $0 \le x < 180$.

  1. $\tan x = 2$, so $x = \tan^{-1}2 \approx 63.4^\circ$
  2. Adding $180^\circ$ gives $243.4^\circ$, outside the range

5

The method

  1. Expand the compound angle

    Use $\sin(x + 30^\circ) = \sin x\cos 30^\circ + \cos x\sin 30^\circ$.

  2. Collect the terms

    Gather all $\sin x$ terms on one side and all $\cos x$ terms on the other.

  3. Form the tangent

    Divide by $\cos x$ to get $\tan x = k$.

  4. Find all solutions

    Use $\tan^{-1}$, then add $180^\circ$ for the next solution in range.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Inverted ratio

    $\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$, not $\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}$.

  • ✗
    Missing the second root

    Tangent has a solution every half-turn, so check the range for each.

Solve the equation $\sin(2\theta - 20)^\circ - 3\cos(2\theta - 20)^\circ = 0$ for $0 \le \theta \le 180$.

Worked explanation

  1. $\sin(2\theta - 20)^\circ = 3\cos(2\theta - 20)^\circ \Rightarrow \tan(2\theta - 20)^\circ = 3$
  2. Let $u = 2\theta - 20$. Since $0 \le \theta \le 180$: $-20 \le u \le 340$.
  3. $u = \tan^{-1}3 = 71.565^\circ$ or $u = 71.565^\circ + 180^\circ = 251.565^\circ$
  4. $2\theta = 91.565 \Rightarrow \theta = 45.8$; $2\theta = 271.565 \Rightarrow \theta = 135.8$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$\theta = 45.8^\circ$ or $\theta = 135.8^\circ$ (1 d.p.)

How the marks are earned

A method mark for reaching tan(2 theta - 20) = 3, a method mark for the principal value, a method mark for adding 180 degrees, and accuracy marks for both values of theta.

Calculation check

  • round(71.56505117707799,3) = 71.565
  • round((71.56505117707799+20) ÷ 2,1) = 45.8
  • round((71.56505117707799+180+20) ÷ 2,1) = 135.8
