Sum and product of roots

Writing down alpha + beta and alpha*beta from the coefficients and using them to find expressions such as alpha^2 + beta^2, alpha^3 + beta^3, alpha^4 + beta^4 or fractions in the roots, without solving the equation.

Type 3 2 The quadratic function FPM-QUA-07

★★★★★ · 13 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Sum and product of roots

Calculation

For $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with roots $\alpha$ and $\beta$: $\alpha+\beta=-\frac{b}{a}$ and $\alpha\beta=\frac{c}{a}$. Build any symmetric expression from these two, without solving.

Find the sum and product before the identity.

  1. Sum is minus b over a
  2. Product is c over a
  3. Final value 34/9

Sum is minus b over a

Trap
Keeping $+\frac{b}{a}$ gives $-\frac{2}{3}$, which is wrong.

Product is c over a

Working
$\frac{c}{a}=\frac{-5}{3}$, with the sign kept.

Final value 34/9

5

The method

  1. Sum and product

    Write $\alpha+\beta=-\frac{b}{a}$ and $\alpha\beta=\frac{c}{a}$, with their signs.

  2. Choose the identity

    Use $\alpha^2+\beta^2=(\alpha+\beta)^2-2\alpha\beta$, or the cube form.

  3. Substitute

    Put in the values exactly, keeping fractions.

  4. Simplify

    Combine into one fraction and cancel.

  5. Check the signs

    Check each sign and the size of the answer.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Sign of the sum

    $-\frac{b}{a}$ keeps the minus sign, even when $b$ is negative.

  • ✗
    Missing factor

    In the cube identity, $\alpha+\beta$ multiplies $3\alpha\beta$.

What a complete response includes

A mark for the correct sum and product, a method mark for a correct identity with values substituted, and an accuracy mark for the exact value. In show-that parts the marks are for each necessary line, and the final mark needs the given answer reached with no errors.

Sum and product of roots · June 2023 · Paper 2 · Question 11 · part (b)(i) · 2 marks

The roots of a quadratic equation $E$ are $\alpha$ and $\beta$ where $\alpha>\beta>0$. Given that $\alpha-\beta=2\sqrt{6}$ and $\alpha^2+\beta^2=30$, and having shown in part (a) that $\alpha\beta=3$ and $\alpha+\beta=6$, without solving $E$ find the value of $\alpha^4+\beta^4$. (2 marks)

Worked explanation

  1. $\alpha^4+\beta^4=(\alpha^2+\beta^2)^2-2(\alpha\beta)^2$
  2. $=30^2-2(3)^2=900-18$
  3. $=882$
  4. Check: the roots are $3\pm\sqrt{6}$ and $(3+\sqrt{6})^4+(3-\sqrt{6})^4=882$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$\alpha^4+\beta^4=882$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for using $(\alpha^2+\beta^2)^2-2(\alpha\beta)^2$ with the values substituted, and an accuracy mark for 882.

Calculation check

  • 30 × × 2-2 × 3 × × 2 = 882
  • round((3+6 × × 0.5) × × 4+(3-6 × × 0.5) × × 4,6) = 882
