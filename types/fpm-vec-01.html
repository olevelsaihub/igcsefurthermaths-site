Getting from A to B: subtracting position vectors

Finding the vector joining two points, or the position vector of a point, by subtracting or adding position vectors given in i, j or in letter vectors such as a and b.

Type 1 7 Vectors FPM-VEC-01

★★★☆☆ · 8 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

The question gives position vectors (for example 'the point $A$ has position vector $4\mathbf{i}+3\mathbf{j}$', or $\overrightarrow{OA}=\mathbf{a}$) and asks you to 'Find $\overrightarrow{AB}$' or 'Find the position vector of $B$', usually for 1 or 2 marks as part (a) of a vectors question.

Getting from A to B: subtracting position vectors

Calculation

The vector from $A$ to $B$ is end minus start: $\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{OB}-\overrightarrow{OA}$. Subtract the $\mathbf{i}$ parts and the $\mathbf{j}$ parts separately, keeping brackets.

5

The method

  1. Write end minus start

    Use $\overrightarrow{PQ}=\overrightarrow{OQ}-\overrightarrow{OP}$.

  2. Add brackets

    Keep each position vector in brackets before subtracting.

  3. Subtract the parts

    Subtract the $\mathbf{i}$ parts, then the $\mathbf{j}$ parts.

  4. Position vector instead

    Add the displacement to the start: $\overrightarrow{OB}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\overrightarrow{AB}$.

  5. Give the form asked

    Write it in $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$ form, or as the letter vector asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong direction

    $\overrightarrow{QP}$ is the negative of $\overrightarrow{PQ}$.

  • ✗
    Dropped brackets

    Every part of the second vector changes sign when you subtract it.

What a complete response includes

Usually 2 marks: a method mark for a correct subtraction (or addition) set up in the right order, and an accuracy mark for the correct simplified vector. A reversed subtraction normally earns no accuracy mark.

Getting from A to B: subtracting position vectors · June 2019 · Paper 2 · Question 1 · part (a) · 2 marks

Referred to a fixed origin $O$, the point $A$ has position vector $(4\mathbf{i}+3\mathbf{j})$ and the point $B$ has position vector $(\mathbf{i}+7\mathbf{j})$. (a) Find $\overrightarrow{AB}$ as a simplified expression in terms of $\mathbf{i}$ and $\mathbf{j}$. (2 marks)

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $\overrightarrow{AB}=\overrightarrow{OB}-\overrightarrow{OA}$
  2. $\overrightarrow{AB}=(\mathbf{i}+7\mathbf{j})-(4\mathbf{i}+3\mathbf{j})$
  3. $\overrightarrow{AB}=(1-4)\mathbf{i}+(7-3)\mathbf{j}$
  4. $\overrightarrow{AB}=-3\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$\overrightarrow{AB}=-3\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}$

How the marks are earned

One method mark for writing $\overrightarrow{OB}-\overrightarrow{OA}$ with the given vectors (the right way round), and one accuracy mark for $-3\mathbf{i}+4\mathbf{j}$.

Calculation check

  • 1-4 = -3
  • 7-3 = 4
