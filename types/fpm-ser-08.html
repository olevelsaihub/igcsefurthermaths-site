The method

  1. Write terms

    Use $ar^{n-1}$ for each given term.

  2. Divide

    Divide the later term by the earlier one.

  3. Solve for r

    Take the root the condition allows.

  4. Find a

    Substitute $r$ back into one equation.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Power error

    The $n$th term is $ar^{n-1}$, not $ar^n$.

  • ✗
    Missed root

    A condition decides between $\pm$ values.

Finding the first term and common ratio of a geometric series · June 2025 · Paper 2 · Question 4 · part (a)(ii) · 5 marks

A geometric series $G$ has first term $a$ and common ratio $r$. The third term of $G$ is $10$ and the seventh term of $G$ is $40$. Given that the second term of $G$ is negative, find (i) the exact value of $r$, (ii) the value of $a$.

Worked explanation

  1. $ar^2 = 10$ and $ar^6 = 40$
  2. Divide: $\frac{ar^6}{ar^2} = \frac{40}{10} \Rightarrow r^4 = 4$
  3. $r^2 = 2$ (as $r^2$ cannot be $-2$), so $r = \pm\sqrt{2}$
  4. Second term $ar = \frac{ar^2}{r} = \frac{10}{r}$ is negative, so $r < 0$: $r = -\sqrt{2}$
  5. $a = \frac{10}{r^2} = \frac{10}{2} = 5$
  6. Check: $ar^6 = 5 \times 8 = 40$ and $ar = -5\sqrt{2} < 0$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

(i) $r = -\sqrt{2}$ (ii) $a = 5$

How the marks are earned

A method mark for writing both terms as ar^2 and ar^6, a method mark for dividing to get r^4 = 4, an accuracy mark for r = -sqrt(2) with the sign justified by the negative second term, then a method mark for substituting back and an accuracy mark for a = 5.

Calculation check

  • 40 ÷ 10 = 4
  • 4 × × 0.5 = 2
  • 10 ÷ 2 = 5
  • 5 × 2 × × 3 = 40
  • round(5 × (-(2 × × 0.5)),4) = -7.0711
