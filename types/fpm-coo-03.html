Writing the equation of a line through two points

Finding the gradient from two points (or from a gradient and one point) and writing the straight-line equation in the form the question asks for.

Type 4 8 Coordinate geometry FPM-COO-03

★★★★☆ · 10 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

'Find an equation of the line through $A$ and $B$', often part (a), with a required form such as $ax + by + c = 0$ where $a$, $b$, $c$ are integers, or $y = mx + c$.

Knowledge card

Writing the equation of a line through two points

Calculation

The gradient is $\frac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}$. Use it in $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$, then clear fractions into the form asked.

Why
$y - (-4)$ is $y + 4$.

Trap

The calculation

$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$, and $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$

$y = mx + c$: $m$ is the gradient and $c$ is the $y$-intercept

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Inverting the gradient: $\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta y}$ is wrong
  • Writing $x - 6$ for $x - (-6)$

Try itFind the line through $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 11)$ in the form $ax + by + c = 0$.

  1. $m = \frac{11 - 2}{4 - 1} = 3$, so $y - 2 = 3(x - 1)$
  2. $3x - y - 1 = 0$

5

The method

  1. Gradient

    Find $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ with the points in the same order top and bottom.

  2. Point-gradient form

    Substitute $m$ and one point into $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$.

  3. Clear fractions

    Multiply through and rearrange into the form asked, with integers.

  4. Check

    Substitute the other point.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Inverted gradient

    $\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta y}$ is not the gradient.

  • ✗
    Sign slips

    Write $x + 2$ for $x - (-2)$, not $x - 2$.

What a complete response includes

Typically a method mark for the gradient, a method mark for a correct line through a known point using that gradient, and an accuracy mark for the equation in the required form. In two-mark versions the gradient and line method are combined into one method mark.

Writing the equation of a line through two points · June 2023 · Paper 2 · Question 8 · part (a) · 3 marks

The points $A$ and $B$ have coordinates $(-6, 8)$ and $(12, 2)$ respectively. (a) Find an equation of the straight line passing through $A$ and $B$ in the form $ax + by + c = 0$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers to be found.

Worked explanation

  1. $m = \frac{2 - 8}{12 - (-6)} = \frac{-6}{18} = -\frac{1}{3}$
  2. $y - 8 = -\frac{1}{3}(x - (-6)) = -\frac{1}{3}(x + 6)$
  3. Multiply by 3: $3y - 24 = -x - 6$
  4. $x + 3y - 18 = 0$
  5. Check with $B(12, 2)$: $12 + 3(2) - 18 = 0$ ✓
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

$x + 3y - 18 = 0$ (so $a = 1$, $b = 3$, $c = -18$, or any integer multiple)

How the marks are earned

A method mark for the gradient $-\frac{1}{3}$, a method mark for using it with one of the points in a line equation, and an accuracy mark for an integer form such as $x + 3y - 18 = 0$.

Calculation check

  • round((2-8) ÷ (12-(-6)),6) = -0.333333
  • -6+3 × 8-18 = 0
  • 12+3 × 2-18 = 0
