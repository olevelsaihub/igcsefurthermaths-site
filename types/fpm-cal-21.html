Areas of regions made of several pieces

Finding the area of a region built from an integral plus or minus a triangle, or from two curves each on its own interval.

Type 28 9 Calculus FPM-CAL-21

★★☆☆☆ · 6 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Very Difficult.

'The finite region $R$ is bounded by $C$, $l$ and the $y$-axis' (or 'the curve, the line, the $x$-axis and $x = 1$'), where the sketch shows a region whose boundary is made of different curves and lines.

Knowledge card

Areas of regions made of several pieces

Calculation

A boundary that changes part-way must be split into pieces: use an integral for a curve and a triangle for a straight line.

3 steps to find

Which piece is a triangle, and which needs an integral?

  1. Tangent meets the y-axis at 8
  2. Straight piece: a triangle
  3. Subtract the curved piece

Tangent meets the y-axis at 8

Trap
The line is not $y = 4$ at $x = 0$: it is $y = 8$.

Straight piece: a triangle

Why
A triangle is quicker than an integral for a straight line.

Subtract the curved piece

Working
$R$ is the triangle minus the area under the curve.
0 of 3 found

2 steps to find

Check the gradient before writing the line.

  1. Gradient from the curve
  2. Line through (2, 0)

Gradient from the curve

Working
Differentiate $4 - x^2$ to get $-2x$.

Line through (2, 0)

Check
At x = 2: 8 − 8 = 0, so the line passes through (2, 0).
0 of 2 found
2

The calculation

$\text{Area} = \text{triangle} \pm \int_a^b y\,dx$ for the curved pieces

Triangle area is $\frac{1}{2}bh$; find every corner from intersections and axis intercepts first.

Tap a part to see what it means and how to find it.

  • Missing the triangle, often the piece below the $x$-axis
  • Using the wrong intercept of the line

Try itFind the area between $y = 2x + 1$, the $x$-axis and the lines $x = 0$ and $x = 3$.

  1. Trapezium with parallel sides $1$ and $7$ and width $3$: $\frac{1}{2}(1 + 7) \times 3 = 12$
  2. Check: $\int_0^3 (2x + 1)\,dx = \left[x^2 + x\right]_0^3 = 12$

5

The method

  1. Sketch and mark corners

    Find where each boundary meets the others and the axes.

  2. Split the region

    Triangles for straight pieces, integrals for curved pieces.

  3. Find each piece

    Use $\frac{1}{2}bh$ or integrate between the limits.

  4. Add or subtract

    Use the sketch to decide which pieces to add.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong intercept

    A line through $(2, 0)$ may meet the $y$-axis at a different point.

  • ✗
    Missed piece

    Count every piece of the boundary, including any triangle.

Areas of regions made of several pieces · January 2023 · Paper 2 · Question 11 · part (c) · 7 marks

The curve $C$ has equation $y = 4 - e^{2x}$. $C$ crosses the $y$-axis at $A(0, 3)$ and the $x$-axis at $B(\ln 2, 0)$. The line $l$ is the normal to $C$ at $B$ and has equation $y = \frac{1}{8}x - \frac{1}{8}\ln 2$. The finite region $R$ is bounded by $C$, $l$ and the $y$-axis. Using calculus, find the area of $R$, giving your answer to one decimal place.

Worked explanation

  1. The region has corners $A(0, 3)$, $B(\ln 2, 0)$ and $D\left(0, -\frac{1}{8}\ln 2\right)$, where $l$ meets the $y$-axis.
  2. Split along the $x$-axis: the part above it is under $C$ from $0$ to $\ln 2$; the part below it is the triangle $OBD$.
  3. $\int_0^{\ln 2}(4 - e^{2x})\,dx = \left[4x - \frac{1}{2}e^{2x}\right]_0^{\ln 2} = (4\ln 2 - 2) - \left(0 - \frac{1}{2}\right) = 4\ln 2 - \frac{3}{2} = 1.2726\ldots$
  4. Triangle $OBD$: $\frac{1}{2} \times \ln 2 \times \frac{1}{8}\ln 2 = \frac{(\ln 2)^2}{16} = 0.0300\ldots$
  5. Area of $R = 4\ln 2 - \frac{3}{2} + \frac{(\ln 2)^2}{16} = 1.3026\ldots$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Area of $R \approx 1.3$ (1 d.p.)

How the marks are earned

A method mark for integrating $4 - e^{2x}$, a method mark for using the limits $0$ and $\ln 2$, a method mark for the triangle below the axis (using the $y$-intercept of $l$), a method mark for adding the pieces, and accuracy for 1.3.

Calculation check

  • round(4 × 0.6931471805599453 - 1.5, 4) = 1.2726
  • round(0.6931471805599453 × × 2 ÷ 16, 4) = 0.03
  • round(4 × 0.6931471805599453 - 1.5 + 0.6931471805599453 × × 2 ÷ 16, 1) = 1.3
