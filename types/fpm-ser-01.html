Finding the first term and common difference of an arithmetic series

Calculation

An arithmetic series adds the same difference $d$ each time. Two known terms give two equations in $a$ and $d$, which are solved together.

The calculation

$u_n = a + (n-1)d$

Sum: $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$

  • Using $a + nd$ for the $n$th term

5

The method

  1. Write each term

    Use $a + (n-1)d$ for every term you are given.

  2. Set up two equations

    Each fact gives one equation in $a$ and $d$.

  3. Solve together

    Subtracting often removes $a$ at once.

  4. Substitute

    Use $a$ and $d$ for the term or sum asked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong nth term

    $a + nd$ is wrong: use $a + (n-1)d$.

  • ✗
    Skipped check

    Put $a$ and $d$ back into both facts.

What a complete response includes

Method marks are given for writing each condition correctly in terms of a and d and for a correct method of solving the pair of equations; accuracy marks follow for a and d. If a further term or sum is asked for, there is a method mark for using the correct formula with their a and d and an accuracy mark for the value.

Finding the first term and common difference of an arithmetic series · November 2024 · Paper 1 · Question 2 · part (a) · 5 marks

The sum of the fifth, sixth and seventh terms of an arithmetic series $A$ is nine times the sum of the first and second terms. The third term of $A$ is $12$. (a) Find the first term and common difference of $A$.

Worked explanation

  1. Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference be $d$.
  2. $u_5 + u_6 + u_7 = (a + 4d) + (a + 5d) + (a + 6d) = 3a + 15d$
  3. $u_1 + u_2 = a + (a + d) = 2a + d$
  4. $3a + 15d = 9(2a + d) \Rightarrow 3a + 15d = 18a + 9d \Rightarrow 6d = 15a \Rightarrow d = \frac{5a}{2}$
  5. Third term: $u_3 = a + 2d = 12$
  6. Substitute: $a + 2\left(\frac{5a}{2}\right) = 12 \Rightarrow 6a = 12 \Rightarrow a = 2$
  7. $d = \frac{5 \times 2}{2} = 5$
  8. Check: $u_5 + u_6 + u_7 = 22 + 27 + 32 = 81$ and $9(u_1 + u_2) = 9(2 + 7) = 81$. Also $u_3 = 2 + 10 = 12$.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

First term $a = 2$, common difference $d = 5$

How the marks are earned

Credit is given for writing the sum of the three terms and the sum of the first two terms correctly in a and d, for setting up the 'nine times' equation, for the third-term equation, and for solving the pair; the final accuracy marks are for a = 2 and d = 5.

Calculation check

  • (2+4 × 5)+(2+5 × 5)+(2+6 × 5) = 81
  • 9 × (2+(2+5)) = 81
  • 2+2 × 5 = 12
