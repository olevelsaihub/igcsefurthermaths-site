Finding a route: vectors round a shape with midpoints and ratios

Building a vector in terms of given base vectors by travelling along known sides of a figure, including through midpoints and points that divide a line in a given ratio.

Type 3 7 Vectors FPM-VEC-04

★★★★★ · 13 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

A diagram of a triangle, parallelogram, trapezium or hexagon with two sides labelled as vectors (for example $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$), midpoints or ratios such as $ON:NB=3:1$, and the instruction 'Find, in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, $\overrightarrow{AN}$'.

Knowledge card

Finding a route: vectors round a shape with midpoints and ratios

Calculation

Find any vector by travelling along known vectors. A midpoint is half the way along a line, and a ratio $m:n$ means the fraction $\frac{m}{m+n}$ of the way from the start.

Build the route, one known vector at a time

3 steps to find

Find OM when M is the midpoint of AB.

  1. Midpoint is half of AB
  2. Route from O through A
  3. Two halves

Midpoint is half of AB

Why
$M$ is halfway, so $\overrightarrow{AM}=\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$.

Route from O through A

Working
$\overrightarrow{OM}=\overrightarrow{OA}+\overrightarrow{AM}$.

Two halves

Check
The midpoint of $AB$ is $\frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b})$.

Ratio becomes a fraction

Trap
$\frac{2}{3}$ is wrong: the fraction is $\frac{m}{m+n}=\frac{2}{5}$.

The calculation

If $AP:PB=m:n$, then $\overrightarrow{AP}=\frac{m}{m+n}\overrightarrow{AB}$.

Try it$OA=\mathbf{a}$, $OB=\mathbf{b}$, and $N$ lies on $AB$ with $AN:NB=1:3$. Find $\overrightarrow{ON}$.

  1. $\overrightarrow{AN}=\frac{1}{4}(\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})$
  2. $\overrightarrow{ON}=\mathbf{a}+\frac{1}{4}(\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a})=\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{a}+\frac{1}{4}\mathbf{b}$

5

The method

  1. Mark known vectors

    Put on the diagram every vector you know, including midpoint and ratio fractions.

  2. Turn ratios into fractions

    Use $\frac{m}{m+n}$ of the line from the start point.

  3. Choose a route

    Travel from the start to the end along known vectors.

  4. Reverse signs

    Put a minus sign on any step taken against the arrow.

  5. Collect terms

    Add the $\mathbf{a}$ parts and the $\mathbf{b}$ parts.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong ratio

    $m:n$ gives $\frac{m}{m+n}$, not $\frac{m}{n}$.

  • ✗
    Forgotten minus

    Going backwards along a vector changes its sign.

Finding a route: vectors round a shape with midpoints and ratios · January 2023 · Paper 1 · Question 11 · part (a)(i) · 3 marks

Triangle $OAB$ has $\overrightarrow{OA}=\mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB}=\mathbf{b}$. $M$ is the midpoint of $OA$. $N$ is the point on $OB$ such that $ON:NB=3:1$. The lines $AN$ and $BM$ intersect at the point $X$. (a) Find expressions, in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, for (i) $\overrightarrow{AN}$ (ii) $\overrightarrow{BM}$. (3 marks)

Worked explanation

  1. $\overrightarrow{ON}=\frac{3}{3+1}\overrightarrow{OB}=\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}$, $\quad \overrightarrow{OM}=\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}$
  2. (i) $\overrightarrow{AN}=\overrightarrow{AO}+\overrightarrow{ON}=-\mathbf{a}+\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}$
  3. (ii) $\overrightarrow{BM}=\overrightarrow{BO}+\overrightarrow{OM}=-\mathbf{b}+\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}=\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

(i) $\overrightarrow{AN}=\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}$ (ii) $\overrightarrow{BM}=\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}$

How the marks are earned

A mark for using $\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}$ (the correct fraction of $OB$) in a route for $\overrightarrow{AN}$, an accuracy mark for $\overrightarrow{AN}$, and a mark for the correct $\overrightarrow{BM}$.

Calculation check

  • 3 ÷ (3+1) = 0.75
  • 2+(0.75 × 4-2) = 3.0
  • 4+(0.5 × 2-4) = 1.0
