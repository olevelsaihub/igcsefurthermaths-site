Finding a route: vectors round a shape with midpoints and ratios

Building a vector in terms of given base vectors by travelling along known sides of a figure, including through midpoints and points that divide a line in a given ratio.

Type 3 7 Vectors FPM-VEC-04

★★★★★ · 13 of 44 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Knowledge card

Finding a route: vectors round a shape with midpoints and ratios

Written
  • Any vector can be found by travelling along known vectors: $\overrightarrow{AN}=\overrightarrow{AO}+\overrightarrow{ON}$.
  • Going backwards along a vector changes its sign: $\overrightarrow{AO}=-\overrightarrow{OA}=-\mathbf{a}$.
  • The midpoint $M$ of $OA$ gives $\overrightarrow{OM}=\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}$.
  • If $ON:NB=3:1$, then $N$ is $\frac{3}{3+1}=\frac{3}{4}$ of the way from $O$ to $B$, so $\overrightarrow{ON}=\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}$.
  • In general, if $P$ divides $AB$ in the ratio $m:n$, then $\overrightarrow{AP}=\frac{m}{m+n}\overrightarrow{AB}$.
  • Equal and parallel sides (e.g. in a parallelogram or regular hexagon) are equal vectors.

Before you begin

  • Adding and subtracting vectors; negative of a vector.
  • Ratio and fractions of a quantity.
  • Collecting like terms with fractions.
  • Properties of parallelograms, trapeziums and regular hexagons.
1

The method

  1. Mark on the diagram every vector you know, including fractions of lines from midpoints and ratios.

    Why: You can only travel along vectors you know.

  2. Turn each ratio into a fraction of the whole line: $m:n$ means $\frac{m}{m+n}$ of the line from the first point.

    Why: A ratio compares two parts, but a vector needs a fraction of the whole.

  3. Choose a route from the start point to the end point along known vectors.

    Why: Any route gives the same answer, so pick the shortest.

  4. Add the steps, putting a minus sign on any step taken against the direction of the arrow.

    Why: Reversing direction gives the negative vector.

  5. Collect like terms and simplify.

    Why: The accuracy mark needs a simplified expression.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Using $\frac{m}{n}$ instead of $\frac{m}{m+n}$, e.g. $\frac{3}{1}\mathbf{b}$ or $\frac{2}{3}$ for a $2:3$ ratio instead of $\frac{2}{5}$.
  • ✗
    Taking the fraction from the wrong end of the line.
  • ✗
    Forgetting to reverse the sign when travelling backwards along a vector.
  • ✗
    Applying a fraction to the wrong vector, e.g. $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{b}$ for the midpoint of $AB$ instead of $\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$.

Finding a route: vectors round a shape with midpoints and ratios · January 2023 · Paper 1 · Question 11 · part (a)(i) · 3 marks

Triangle $OAB$ has $\overrightarrow{OA}=\mathbf{a}$ and $\overrightarrow{OB}=\mathbf{b}$. $M$ is the midpoint of $OA$. $N$ is the point on $OB$ such that $ON:NB=3:1$. The lines $AN$ and $BM$ intersect at the point $X$. (a) Find expressions, in terms of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$, for (i) $\overrightarrow{AN}$ (ii) $\overrightarrow{BM}$. (3 marks)

Question restated by olevels.com from the original paper; the original is below.

View full original question and diagrams
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. $\overrightarrow{ON}=\frac{3}{3+1}\overrightarrow{OB}=\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}$, $\quad \overrightarrow{OM}=\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}$
  2. (i) $\overrightarrow{AN}=\overrightarrow{AO}+\overrightarrow{ON}=-\mathbf{a}+\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}$
  3. (ii) $\overrightarrow{BM}=\overrightarrow{BO}+\overrightarrow{OM}=-\mathbf{b}+\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}=\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

(i) $\overrightarrow{AN}=\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}-\mathbf{a}$ (ii) $\overrightarrow{BM}=\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}$

How the marks are earned

A mark for using $\frac{3}{4}\mathbf{b}$ (the correct fraction of $OB$) in a route for $\overrightarrow{AN}$, an accuracy mark for $\overrightarrow{AN}$, and a mark for the correct $\overrightarrow{BM}$.

Calculation check

  • 3 ÷ (3+1) = 0.75
  • 2+(0.75 × 4-2) = 3.0
  • 4+(0.5 × 2-4) = 1.0
