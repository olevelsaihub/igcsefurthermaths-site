The method

  1. Identify corners

    Read or solve for each corner of R.

  2. Substitute

    Put each corner into P.

  3. Compare

    Pick the greatest or least value.

  4. Integer check

    If x and y must be integers, test the points near the best corner.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong corner

    Corners come from the correct boundary lines.

  • ✗
    Value or point

    State the value of P and where it occurs.

What a complete response includes

A method mark for finding (or reading) the corner coordinates, a method mark for substituting corners into P or using an objective line, and an accuracy mark for the correct value. The 4-mark versions often give an extra mark for solving simultaneous equations to locate a corner exactly.

Greatest or least value in a region · June 2023 · Paper 2 · Question 5 · part (c) · 3 marks

Region $R$ is defined by $y\leqslant 2x+5$, $4y\geqslant x-8$ and $5y+3x\leqslant 30$. For all points in $R$ with coordinates $(x,y)$, $P=2x-5y$. Using your graph, find the least value of $P$.

Worked explanation

  1. Corners of $R$:
  2. (i) and (ii): $4(2x+5)=x-8 \Rightarrow 7x=-28 \Rightarrow x=-4,\ y=-3$
  3. (i) and (iii): $5(2x+5)+3x=30 \Rightarrow 13x=5 \Rightarrow x=\frac{5}{13},\ y=\frac{75}{13}$
  4. (ii) and (iii): $5\cdot\frac{x-8}{4}+3x=30 \Rightarrow 17x=160 \Rightarrow x=\frac{160}{17},\ y=\frac{6}{17}$
  5. $P$ at $(-4,-3)$: $2(-4)-5(-3)=7$
  6. $P$ at $\left(\frac{5}{13},\frac{75}{13}\right)$: $\frac{10}{13}-\frac{375}{13}=-\frac{365}{13}\approx-28.1$
  7. $P$ at $\left(\frac{160}{17},\frac{6}{17}\right)$: $\frac{320}{17}-\frac{30}{17}=\frac{290}{17}\approx 17.1$
  8. The least value is at the top corner: $P=-\frac{365}{13}\approx -28.1$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Least value of $P=-\frac{365}{13}\approx-28.1$ (a value read from an accurate graph, such as about $-28$, comes from the same corner).

How the marks are earned

A method mark for locating the top corner of $R$ (reading about $(0.4,5.8)$ or solving the two equations), a method mark for substituting the corners into $P$, and an accuracy mark for the least value of about $-28.1$.
