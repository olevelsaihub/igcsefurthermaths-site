Knowledge card

Greatest or least value in a region

Calculation
  • For a linear expression such as $P=2x-5y$, the greatest and least values over a region bounded by straight lines occur at corners (vertices) of the region.
  • Find each corner either by reading the graph or, more accurately, by solving the two line equations that meet there simultaneously.
  • Substitute every corner into the expression and compare the results.
  • If the question says x and y are integers, test the integer points inside R near the best corner instead of the corner itself.
  • An alternative is to draw a line P = constant and slide it across R; the last point it touches gives the greatest or least value.

Before you begin

  • Shading the region R (FPM-IDN-06)
  • Solving two linear simultaneous equations
  • Substituting into an expression with negative numbers
1

The method

  1. Identify the corners of R from your graph.

    Why: The optimum of a linear expression is always at a corner, so these are the only candidates.

  2. Find each corner's coordinates exactly by solving the pair of line equations, or read them carefully from the grid.

    Why: Accurate coordinates give an accurate value of P.

  3. Substitute each corner into P.

    Why: Comparing the values shows which corner is best.

  4. If integer coordinates are required, list the integer points in R near the best corner and test those.

    Why: A corner with fractional coordinates is not allowed in that case.

  5. State the greatest or least value as required.

    Why: The accuracy mark is for the value of P, not the point.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Using a corner of the wrong region because the shading was wrong.
  • ✗
    Reading corner coordinates inaccurately when the corner is not at a grid point.
  • ✗
    Ignoring an 'integer values' condition.
  • ✗
    Sign errors when substituting negative coordinates, e.g. -5 times -3.
  • ✗
    Giving the coordinates of the corner instead of the value of P.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from typical examiner credit patterns for this method; not measured student-error statistics.

Now see it used on a real past-paper question

What a complete response includes

A method mark for finding (or reading) the corner coordinates, a method mark for substituting corners into P or using an objective line, and an accuracy mark for the correct value. The 4-mark versions often give an extra mark for solving simultaneous equations to locate a corner exactly.

Greatest or least value in a region · June 2023 · Paper 2 · Question 5 · part (c) · 3 marks

Region $R$ is defined by $y\leqslant 2x+5$, $4y\geqslant x-8$ and $5y+3x\leqslant 30$. For all points in $R$ with coordinates $(x,y)$, $P=2x-5y$. Using your graph, find the least value of $P$.

Question restated by olevels.com from the original paper; the original is below.

View full original question and diagrams
After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Corners of $R$:
  2. (i) and (ii): $4(2x+5)=x-8 \Rightarrow 7x=-28 \Rightarrow x=-4,\ y=-3$
  3. (i) and (iii): $5(2x+5)+3x=30 \Rightarrow 13x=5 \Rightarrow x=\frac{5}{13},\ y=\frac{75}{13}$
  4. (ii) and (iii): $5\cdot\frac{x-8}{4}+3x=30 \Rightarrow 17x=160 \Rightarrow x=\frac{160}{17},\ y=\frac{6}{17}$
  5. $P$ at $(-4,-3)$: $2(-4)-5(-3)=7$
  6. $P$ at $\left(\frac{5}{13},\frac{75}{13}\right)$: $\frac{10}{13}-\frac{375}{13}=-\frac{365}{13}\approx-28.1$
  7. $P$ at $\left(\frac{160}{17},\frac{6}{17}\right)$: $\frac{320}{17}-\frac{30}{17}=\frac{290}{17}\approx 17.1$
  8. The least value is at the top corner: $P=-\frac{365}{13}\approx -28.1$
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Least value of $P=-\frac{365}{13}\approx-28.1$ (a value read from an accurate graph, such as about $-28$, comes from the same corner).

How the marks are earned

A method mark for locating the top corner of $R$ (reading about $(0.4,5.8)$ or solving the two equations), a method mark for substituting the corners into $P$, and an accuracy mark for the least value of about $-28.1$.

Calculation check
