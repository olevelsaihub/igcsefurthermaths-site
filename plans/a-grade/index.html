Before you start: supporting skills

These starting skills support many types. Check them first, and come back to them whenever a type feels hard.

  • Reading a Further Pure question: Tell 'show that' (reach the printed result with every step shown) from 'find', 'solve' and 'hence'; give answers in the form asked (exact surd, fraction, ln or pi form, or to 3 significant figures); and use earlier parts and the formula sheet where the question expects it.
  • GCSE algebraic manipulation: Expand brackets, factorise (common factor, quadratics, difference of two squares), simplify and combine algebraic fractions, use the laws of indices including negative and fractional powers, and change the subject of a formula.
  • Solving linear, quadratic and simultaneous equations: Solve linear equations, quadratic equations by factorising and by the formula (leaving exact roots), and a pair of linear simultaneous equations; substitute values accurately and check a solution.
