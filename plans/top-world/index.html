A-Grade Plan · 70 types

Coordinate geometry: Finding the length between two points · Midpoints and points that split a line in a ratio · Writing the equation of a line through two points · Parallel and perpendicular lines: proving it or finding an unknown · Equation of a perpendicular line (including perpendicular bisectors) · Area of a triangle or quadrilateral from coordinates

Differentiation and its applications: Product rule and chain rule: differentiating products and 'function of a function' · The quotient rule: differentiating fractions · Finding stationary points · Maximum or minimum? Using the second derivative · Finding the equation of a tangent · Finding the equation of a normal · Optimisation part 1: building the formula to maximise or minimise · Optimisation part 2: finding, justifying and evaluating the maximum or minimum · Rates of change with one chain-rule link

Identities, equations and inequalities: Drawing the lines for an inequality graph · Factor theorem: show a factor or find a constant · Shading or describing a region · Factorising and solving a cubic · Where a line meets a curve (simultaneous equations) · Quadratic inequalities · Two constants from factor and remainder information

Logarithms, indices and surds: What a log means: reading values and switching to index form · Surds: simplifying and rationalising the denominator · Log equations: combine into one log, then remove the log

Graphs: Writing down the asymptotes of a rational curve · Where a rational curve crosses the axes · Filling in a table of values · Drawing the graph from your table · Sketching a rational curve with its asymptotes · Sketching curves and where they cross the axes · Solving an equation with your graph and a straight line · Solving log and exponential equations with your graph

Series and the binomial expansion: Expanding a bracket with a negative or fractional power · When is a binomial expansion valid? · Getting a bracket ready for the binomial series: a(1 + bx)^n · Finding the first term and common difference of an arithmetic series · Using a binomial expansion to estimate a value · Finding n from an equation linking sums and terms · Finding the first term and common ratio of a geometric series · Does a geometric series have a sum to infinity? · Sum to infinity of a geometric series · Adding up a finite geometric series · Finding n in a geometric series with logarithms

The quadratic function: Completing the square · Reading the maximum or minimum from completed-square form · Sum and product of roots · The discriminant: equal, real or no real roots · Forming a new quadratic from the roots of another

Radians, sectors, triangles and 3D trigonometry: Lengths in a 3D solid using right-angled triangles · Area of a triangle using half ab sin C · Using the cosine rule, including when it gives a quadratic · Using the sine rule, including the two-answer case · Angle between two planes using a right-angled triangle

Trigonometric identities and equations: Deriving the double-angle formulae · Solving trig equations with a multiple or shifted angle · Proving identities with double-angle and addition formulae

Integration, areas, volumes and kinematics: Velocity and acceleration by differentiating · When is the particle at rest (or at a given velocity)? · Integrating trig functions using identities · Volume of revolution about the x-axis · Area between a curve and the x-axis · Area between two curves (or a curve and a line) · Volumes of revolution between two boundaries

Vectors: Getting from A to B: subtracting position vectors · Finding a route: vectors round a shape with midpoints and ratios · How long is a vector? Magnitudes and finding unknowns · Unit vectors: same direction, length one · Proving shapes and straight lines with vectors · Where lines cross: two routes and comparing coefficients
