Completion time

Study 7 types per day, then sit three complete Paper 1 + Paper 2 mock pairs as six separate paper sessions. That is 14 active days.

This keeps the olevels.com Pass Plan pace of about 6.6 types per learning day.

Readiness gate

Completion is practice. Fresh papers confirm readiness.

After completing the plan, attempt three fresh, unseen 200-mark Paper 1 + Paper 2 paper pairs under timed conditions. Have each pair independently marked with its official scheme. Grade 5 readiness is supported only when every score is at least 10 marks above that exact pair’s published Grade 5 boundary.

Supporting skills for Further Pure Mathematics

Use these short bridges when a starting skill needs strengthening.

Reading a Further Pure question

Tell 'show that' (reach the printed result with every step shown) from 'find', 'solve' and 'hence'; give answers in the form asked (exact surd, fraction, ln or pi form, or to 3 significant figures); and use earlier parts and the formula sheet where the question expects it.

GCSE algebraic manipulation

Expand brackets, factorise (common factor, quadratics, difference of two squares), simplify and combine algebraic fractions, use the laws of indices including negative and fractional powers, and change the subject of a formula.

Solving linear, quadratic and simultaneous equations

Solve linear equations, quadratic equations by factorising and by the formula (leaving exact roots), and a pair of linear simultaneous equations; substitute values accurately and check a solution.
