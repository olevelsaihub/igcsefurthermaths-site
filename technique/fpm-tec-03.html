Find and calculate: exact form or the stated accuracy

You must work out a value or expression and give it in the right form. Unless the question says otherwise, give decimal answers to 3 significant figures.

Command words: Find, Calculate, Evaluate, DetermineUsually 2 to 5 marksAbout 1 minute per mark

What Pearson asks

You must work out a value or expression and give it in the right form. Unless the question says otherwise, give decimal answers to 3 significant figures.

How the marks are earned

Method marks are for choosing and using a correct method, even if there is an arithmetic slip. The accuracy mark is for the correct value in the form asked for, so rounding and units matter.

Method

  1. Write down the formula or rule you will use.

    Why: A correct method earns the method mark even if the answer is wrong.

  2. Substitute carefully and show the calculation.

    Why: Working lets you keep method marks after a slip.

  3. Check the form asked for: exact, or a stated accuracy.

    Why: 'Exact' means surds, fractions, $\pi$ or ln, not decimals.

  4. Add units and check the answer is sensible.

    Why: Units are often part of the final mark.

Answer frame

Formula, substitution, calculation, final answer with units, in the form asked.

Common errors

Inferred, not measured: Inferred from how 4PM1 command words are usually credited; not measured student-error statistics.

  • Giving a decimal when an exact answer was asked for.
  • Rounding too early, so the final digits are wrong.
  • Forgetting a part of the answer, such as the radii in a perimeter.
  • Leaving out units.

Worked illustration

January 2022 · Paper 2 · Q1(b)

The complete original question is shown; the illustration answers part (b).

Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Arc $AB = r\theta = 7 \times 1.2 = 8.4$ cm

Perimeter $= OA + OB + \text{arc } AB = 7 + 7 + 8.4$

$= 22.4$ cm

Why it earns the marks

The method mark is for using arc length $r\theta$ and adding the two radii, and the accuracy mark is for $22.4$.
