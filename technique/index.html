Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Further Pure Mathematics · 4PM1

Answer technique cards

How each command word is marked, with a method and an authored worked illustration.

01

Show that: reaching a given result

You are given the final result and must prove it is true using clear working. The answer is printed, so the marks are for the steps.

02

Hence: using the previous part

You must use the result you have just found or been given to answer this part. With 'hence or otherwise' you may use any valid method.

03

Find and calculate: exact form or the stated accuracy

You must work out a value or expression and give it in the right form. Unless the question says otherwise, give decimal answers to 3 significant figures.

04

Solve: every solution in the range

You must find all values that satisfy the equation or inequality. Trig questions give a range in degrees or radians, and every solution in that range is needed.

05

Write down and state: one-step answers

You need to give a result you can see or recall without long working. Little or no calculation is expected.

06

Sketch and draw: what must be labelled

A 'sketch' is a quick graph showing the shape and key features, not exact plotting. A 'draw' needs accurate plotting on the grid provided.

07

Prove: identities and algebraic proofs

You must show that a statement is always true, using logical steps. The same rules as 'show that' apply, with a clear line of reasoning.

08

Express, complete and form: writing in a required form

You must write something in the exact form or layout the question states. The form is part of the answer.
